Who speaks better English?

Talk while the wave dances to your voice — then get scored on fluency, vocabulary, clarity and flow. Play on one phone, or live-call a rival on their own phone.

Works phone-to-phone over the internet. Best with headphones — echo cancellation is on, but speakerphone can still leak a little.

Heads up: live speech recognition needs Chrome or Edge (desktop or Android). On this browser the call will work but scoring may be limited.
📱 iPhone note: the live call and waves work, but Apple's browser usually can't transcribe speech during a call — you'll be scored on voice rhythm without word analysis. For full transcripts and word scoring, use Chrome on Android or a computer.

Speech is transcribed by your browser's built-in recognition. Scoring runs entirely on your device; in call mode your voice goes directly to your rival's phone (peer-to-peer).
