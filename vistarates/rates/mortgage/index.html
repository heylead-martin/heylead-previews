What this number is (and is not)

Freddie Mac's Primary Mortgage Market Survey averages rates offered to borrowers with strong credit and a substantial down payment. It is a market thermometer, not a guaranteed quote.

  • Credit scores below the survey profile usually mean higher rates.
  • Points, lender fees, and discount pricing change the all-in cost.
  • ARMs, FHA, VA, and jumbo loans are different products with different pricing.

How Vistarates handles mortgage monetization

US mortgage affiliate programs are typically paid per lead, not per funded loan, because of licensing and RESPA referral rules. We never claim per-funded-loan compensation for mortgages on this site.
