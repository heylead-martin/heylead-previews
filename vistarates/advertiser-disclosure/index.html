Plain language on compensation, rankings, and what "partner" means on this site.

How we make money

Vistarates receives compensation from some of the companies whose products appear on this site. That compensation may take the form of a cost-per-action (for example, a funded credit card or bank account) or a cost-per-lead (for example, a mortgage inquiry). We may also receive advertising fees for placements that are clearly labeled as such.

Compensation may affect whether a product is eligible to appear and, in limited cases, where it sits in a commercial module. It does not change:

  • The published rate, fee, or bonus numbers
  • The verification date next to those numbers
  • The credit score tier matching on the homepage and card pages
  • Editorial recommendations in guides

Rankings

When we rank products, we use factors you can inspect: APY or APR, annual fee, bonus value after spend requirements, eligibility friction, and data freshness. We do not sell the #1 slot.

Credit score language

For fair and rebuilding credit tiers, call-to-action copy says "See if you prequalify" (or equivalent) rather than hard-sell "Apply now," because soft-pull prequalification reduces unnecessary hard inquiries.

Mortgage offers

Mortgage affiliate relationships, when live, are compensated on a per-lead basis only. We do not claim per-funded-loan pay for mortgages.

Independence

Vistarates is an independent comparison site operated by HeyLead LLC. We are not a bank, lender, credit card issuer, or financial advisor. Always read the provider's official terms before you apply.
