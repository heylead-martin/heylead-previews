Who we are

Vistarates is operated by HeyLead LLC. This policy describes information practices for vistarates.com and staging previews of the same product.

Cookies

We do not require an account. Essential cookies may be set by the host or future consent tools. Affiliate outbound clicks may set third-party cookies on the issuer or network domain after you leave our site.

To operate and secure the site, measure aggregate traffic, send newsletters you requested, and improve content. We do not sell personal information.

Sharing

We use processors such as hosting, email, and analytics vendors. They process data on our instructions. Legal requests and safety exceptions may apply.

Retention

Logs follow host defaults. Newsletter addresses stay until you unsubscribe or we delete inactive lists. You can request deletion of newsletter data via the email footer once live.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We will update this page when practices change and revise the date above.

Contact

Privacy questions: contact details on the about page at public launch. Staging contact is internal only.
