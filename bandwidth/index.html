What this can and cannot see

  • Can: full test uses the official Cloudflare speedtest engine (same ramp as speed.cloudflare.com). Live monitor is a 15 second sample: 6 download streams, upload samples, and a ping every second. It stops on its own. Congestion from other phones, TVs, and downloads shows up as lower Mbps and higher ping.
  • Cannot: list other devices on your Wi-Fi or show per-device usage from a website. Browsers are sandboxed; they cannot sniff LAN traffic.
  • For device discovery on this Mac: run local ~/wifiwatch (python3 wifiwatch.py watch). Real per-device bandwidth needs the router (Ubiquiti, Firewalla, ASUS) or a forced DNS path (NextDNS / Pi-hole).
