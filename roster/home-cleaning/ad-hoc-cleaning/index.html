Ad hoc cleaning in Singapore is a one-time cleaning visit booked when you need it, rather than a weekly or fortnightly plan. It suits a clean before guests arrive, after a busy period or as a trial before committing to regular help. Most companies price it by the hour with a minimum, so plan a clear task list and check the minimum before booking.

Ad hoc cleaning compared with other services

An ad hoc clean is general housekeeping done once. It is lighter than a deep clean and different from a move-out or post-renovation clean, which have specific goals. Choosing the right service helps you get the result you expect without paying for more than you need.

When ad hoc cleaning makes sense

Households book one-time cleans for many reasons: hosting a gathering, recovering from illness, catching up after travel, preparing for a viewing or simply trying a company before a regular plan. It also suits homes that only need help occasionally.

Because the cleaner does not know your home, the first visit takes a little longer. A clear task list and a short walkthrough at the start make a big difference to what gets done in the hours you pay for.

Plan your task list

Rank the tasks by priority, since a few hours rarely covers everything. Be specific about rooms and surfaces.

  • Floors: sweeping, vacuuming and mopping of named rooms.
  • Kitchen: counters, stove top, sink and appliance exteriors.
  • Bathrooms: toilets, basins, showers, mirrors and floors.
  • Dusting: reachable surfaces, shelves and furniture.
  • Beds: changing sheets if fresh linen is ready.
  • Rubbish: emptying bins and taking rubbish to the chute.

Tell the company about surfaces that need care, pets at home, and whether the cleaner should use your products or bring their own. Many companies expect you to supply basic supplies for ad hoc visits, so confirm this when booking.

How many hours to book

The right number of hours depends on the size of the home, how much clutter there is and the tasks on your list. Companies often recommend a minimum by flat type. Ask what the company suggests for your home and list, and book a little extra time for a first visit if the home has not been cleaned for a while.

If you want windows, oven interiors, grout or upholstery cleaned, ask whether these fit in an ad hoc visit or need a separate service. The steam cleaning guide and the sofa and carpet cleaning guide cover specialised cleaning.

How ad hoc cleaning is priced

Most companies charge an hourly rate with a minimum number of hours, and the hourly rate for a one-time visit is often higher than for a regular plan. Some sell ad hoc cleans as a fixed-price visit for a set number of hours or by flat size. Weekend, evening and public holiday visits may cost more.

The published rates further down this page come from company websites in the Roster directory, one matching rate per company with its source and capture date. Compare them on the same unit, and check the source for the minimum hours, supplies and GST.

  • What is the hourly rate, and what is the minimum number of hours?
  • Are cleaning supplies and equipment included?
  • Is there a surcharge for weekends or public holidays?
  • Is there a transport or booking fee?
  • Is GST included?

Booking and access

Agree the date, start time and number of hours, and how the cleaner will enter. If you will not be home, decide how to hand over keys or access, and put valuables away. Agree which rooms are out of bounds.

Ask how the company handles breakages and complaints, and whether it carries insurance for accidental damage. Domestic cleaning is among the activities excluded from NEA's cleaning business licence, so do not rely on a licence as a sign of quality.

Short notice and peak periods

Ad hoc slots can fill up before festive periods such as Chinese New Year and Hari Raya, and on weekends. Book early if your date matters, and ask whether the company charges more for short-notice or peak-period visits.

After the visit

Walk through the home with the cleaner before they leave if you can, and point out anything missed. If you were not home, check soon after and contact the company promptly with any concerns. If the visit went well, ask whether the same cleaner can return for future ad hoc visits or a regular plan.

Ad hoc cleaning questions

What is ad hoc cleaning?

It is a one-time cleaning visit booked when you need it, rather than a regular schedule. It usually covers general housekeeping, such as floors, kitchen and bathrooms, for a set number of hours.

Is ad hoc cleaning more expensive than regular cleaning?

Often the hourly rate is higher for a one-time visit, and the minimum hours may be longer. Compare the published rates and ask each company about its regular rate if you may book again.

Do I need to provide cleaning supplies?

Many companies expect you to supply products and equipment, while some bring their own or charge extra. Confirm before the visit so the cleaner can start straight away.

Can I use an ad hoc clean as a trial?

Yes. Many households book a one-time clean to try a company before committing to a regular plan. Give clear feedback afterwards and ask whether the same cleaner can return.
