Questions

Why is the shortlist empty?

A company needs a phone on its own site or on a Maps page we opened, and a rating we can tie to one page. The cleaning names in the first pass did not clear that.

What would put a cleaner on the list?

A working phone, a page that states a Google rating and a count, and two or three review lines with a name and a date. A marketplace that assigns a different crew each visit stays off the list.

Can I still send a request?

The form is on the page so you can see it. There is no company to receive it, and the beta does not send the form anyway.
