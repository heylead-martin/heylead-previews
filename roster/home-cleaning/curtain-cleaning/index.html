Curtain cleaning in Singapore

Compare curtain cleaning in Singapore. Choose take-down washing, dry cleaning or steam in place, check shrinkage risk and published company rates.

Curtain cleaning in Singapore is usually done either by taking curtains down for washing or dry cleaning, or by steam cleaning them in place on the track. The right method depends on the fabric, the lining and how the curtains are hung. Ask each company which method it uses, whether it removes and rehangs the curtains, and how it prices the job.

Why curtains need professional cleaning

Curtains collect dust, cooking oil and moisture, and in a humid climate they can develop a musty smell or mould spots, especially near windows and in bathrooms or kitchens. Large or lined curtains are hard to wash at home, and some fabrics shrink or lose shape in a home washing machine.

A professional company can clean heavy or delicate curtains with the right method, and many will take them down and rehang them for you. This is often booked during spring cleaning, before Chinese New Year, after renovation or when moving out of a rented home.

Common cleaning methods

Each method has trade-offs. Ask which one the company recommends for your fabric and why.

Check the care label on your curtains and share a photo with the company. Blackout linings, sheers, velvet and curtains with decorative trims may need special care. If the label says dry clean only, ask the company to follow it.

Removal, rehanging and blinds

Taking curtains down and putting them back is part of the job, and it affects both price and timing. Ask whether the company removes and rehangs the curtains, whether it handles hooks and tracks, and how long you will be without curtains. Some companies return curtains within a few days; ask for the expected turnaround.

Blinds are a different job. Roller, Venetian and vertical blinds are usually cleaned in place or replaced, and not every curtain cleaner handles them. If you also need curtain tracks or rods installed or repaired, that is a handyman job rather than a cleaning job.

Shrinkage and fabric risks

Washing can shrink some fabrics or change their colour and texture, and long curtains may hang shorter afterwards. Ask the company how it reduces this risk, whether it measures curtains before cleaning and what happens if they shrink. A careful company will tell you if a curtain is too old or fragile to clean safely.

Old curtains with sun damage, weak stitching or perished linings may tear during cleaning. Point these out before the job starts, and ask the company to note the condition in writing.

How curtain cleaning is priced

Companies price curtain cleaning in several ways: per panel, per piece, by weight, by the size of the window or as part of a bundle with sofa or mattress cleaning. Removal and rehanging may be included or charged separately, and there is often a minimum order.

The published rates further down this page come from company websites in the Roster directory, one matching rate per company with its source and capture date. Because companies price differently, compare the unit carefully and check the source for minimum orders, removal and rehanging, and GST.

  • Is the price per panel, per piece, by weight or per window?
  • Are removal and rehanging included?
  • Is there a minimum order or a transport charge?
  • What is the turnaround time?
  • What happens if a curtain shrinks or is damaged?
  • Is GST included?

Preparing for the visit

Clear the floor and furniture under each window so the team can reach the curtains and tracks. Remove items hanging on curtain rods or tiebacks, and tell the company about motorised tracks or remote-controlled curtains, which may need care when taking down. Count the panels in each room and note any that need special attention, such as a stained lining or a torn hem.

If the curtains are going away for washing, agree the return date and what you will use in the meantime, especially for bedrooms. When they come back, check each panel for length, colour and stains before the team leaves, and make sure hooks and gliders are back in place.

Timing the clean

Plan curtain cleaning around other work. After renovation, clean curtains once dust has settled and the post-renovation clean is done; the post-renovation cleaning guide explains the order. Before moving out, check whether the tenancy agreement requires curtains to be cleaned and keep the receipt; the move-out cleaning guide covers handover requirements.

If you prefer steam cleaning in place, the steam cleaning guide explains drying time and fabric suitability.

Keeping curtains fresh between cleans

Open curtains during the day to let air circulate, and keep windows open where practical to reduce dampness. Vacuum curtains with a soft brush attachment from time to time. Avoid leaving curtains bunched against a wet window or a running aircon unit, which can trap moisture.

Curtain cleaning questions

Can curtains be cleaned without taking them down?

Yes, many companies offer steam cleaning in place. It is less disruptive but may not remove heavy dirt as well as washing. Ask which method suits your curtains and how long they take to dry.

Will my curtains shrink after cleaning?

Some fabrics can shrink when washed. Share the care label with the company and ask how it reduces the risk, whether it measures before cleaning and what happens if shrinkage occurs.

How long does curtain cleaning take?

Steam cleaning in place is usually done in one visit. Take-down washing or dry cleaning may take a few days before the curtains are rehung. Ask the company for its turnaround time.

Does curtain cleaning include blinds?

Not always. Blinds are often a separate service, and some curtain cleaners do not handle them. Ask the company before booking if you also need blinds cleaned.
