Move-out cleaning in Singapore

Prepare for a Singapore home handover with a move-out cleaning checklist, a sensible booking sequence and clear questions about scope and exclusions.

Move-out cleaning in Singapore is a cleaning visit organised around a property handover. The right scope depends on the tenancy requirements, the home's condition and what remains inside. Agree a written checklist and inspection process with the cleaning company, then coordinate the appointment with movers, repairs and the handover date.

Start with the handover requirements

A move-out clean should be based on the property requirements you actually need to meet. Read the relevant handover checklist or tenancy documents and discuss unclear items with the landlord or agent. Do not assume a package advertised as end-of-tenancy cleaning automatically covers every obligation in your agreement.

Turn those requirements into a list for the cleaning company. Separate ordinary cleaning from professional upholstery treatment, aircon servicing, repairs and replacement items. A cleaner can quote more accurately when it knows which documents and tasks you expect from the visit.

The home cleaning directory provides companies to compare. Use the same handover brief for each enquiry so that differences in the quotes reflect a real difference in scope rather than missing information.

Move-out cleaning versus deep cleaning

The main difference is the purpose and agreed checklist. Deep cleaning focuses on a detailed reset, while move-out cleaning is tied to a handover. The actual tasks can overlap substantially, so ask how the company's package fits the condition and requirements of your property.

Prepare a room-by-room handover checklist

A written checklist helps the cleaner and the person inspecting the home work from the same expectations. Identify the rooms, fixtures and accessible storage areas. State whether cupboards, wardrobes and appliances will be emptied before the team arrives.

In the kitchen, specify appliance interiors, cabinetry and grease-related work individually. In bathrooms, list fittings, screens and surfaces, while distinguishing cleaning from damaged sealant or repair work. Include balconies, reachable windows and utility areas if they form part of the agreed scope.

  • List furniture and appliances that remain in the property.
  • Identify items needing specialist care, such as a rug or fabric sofa.
  • Separate existing damage from removable dirt or staining.
  • Record areas excluded because they cannot be safely accessed.
  • Agree who checks the completed work and when.

Avoid treating a cleaning checklist as a promise to restore every worn surface to a new condition. If damage is involved, arrange an assessment by the relevant trade and keep that work separate in the quote.

Put the appointments in a workable order

Cleaning should be scheduled after the work that would undo it, with enough time for a final check. Plan the movers, repairs, aircon servicing and cleaning together. Ask the companies how their work affects access and whether it leaves areas temporarily unavailable.

An empty home may be easier to scope, but confirm that utilities and entry remain available for the appointment. If furniture is staying, tell the company what can be moved and what must remain in place. Do not assume the cleaning team will dismantle or shift heavy items.

Leave a buffer between the clean and the handover when possible. That gives you time to inspect, photograph the result and address any agreed missed tasks. Roster does not have live scheduling information, so confirm every appointment directly with the company.

How move-out cleaning prices are assessed

A useful quote considers property size, condition, furnishing and the actual checklist. A flat advertised by room count can contain very different cleaning work from another of the same type. Share photographs and describe any heavy soiling or unusual access before asking for a fixed amount.

Clarify whether equipment, products, transport and GST are included where applicable. Ask how extra work is priced and who must approve it. If the team discovers a condition that was not visible in photos, the process for revising the quote should already be understood.

Check cancellation, rescheduling and return-visit terms. A package may include a follow-up for missed tasks, but it may not cover new dirt after movers return or a new requirement raised at handover. Obtain the actual conditions rather than relying on a general satisfaction claim.

End-of-tenancy and handover cleaning

End-of-tenancy cleaning is the same job seen from a tenant's side: the home must be returned in the condition the tenancy agreement describes. Read the cleaning clause first, since some agreements ask for professional cleaning or aircon servicing receipts. Share that clause with the company so its checklist matches what the landlord will inspect.

Published end-of-tenancy and move-out rates from directory companies appear further down this page, each with its company, source and capture date. They are advertised starting points, so confirm the scope, the number of cleaners and GST for your unit.

Aircon receipts, upholstery and minor repairs

Additional handover tasks should be booked for their own scope. If your agreement calls for aircon servicing, ask the provider what receipt it issues and keep the date and unit details. The aircon servicing guide explains what to confirm for that visit.

For sofas, carpets or mattresses, check whether specialist treatment is needed and how long the items may be unavailable. Our upholstery guide covers materials, methods and drying arrangements. Include the exact items in the brief instead of assuming a furnished-home clean covers them.

Door adjustments, loose fittings and damaged fixtures are separate repair questions. Use the handyman guide to plan small jobs, while choosing the appropriate specialist for regulated work. Coordinate repairs before final cleaning when the work could create dust or require moving furniture.

Document the result without assuming a deposit outcome

Photographs and receipts create a useful record of the work, but cleaning alone does not determine a tenancy deposit outcome. Keep the agreed checklist, invoice and dated images of the completed rooms. Compare them with the actual handover requirements and discuss any disagreement with the relevant parties.

At the end of the clean, ask the team to identify any task it could not complete and why. Record whether another visit is agreed. A specific note about an inaccessible window or a permanent mark is more useful than a vague statement that everything was finished.

Do not ask a cleaning company to guarantee matters outside its control. It can explain its scope and follow-up policy, while your landlord or agent remains the person to discuss handover acceptance with. Keep those conversations clear and separate.

Move-out cleaning questions

Does the property have to be empty?

Not necessarily, but the company needs to know what remains. Empty and furnished properties can require different work and access arrangements. Confirm whether cupboard interiors, areas behind furniture and appliances are included, and who is responsible for moving or emptying items before the cleaning team arrives.

Can cleaning guarantee that my deposit is returned?

No cleaning package can settle every issue involved in a tenancy handover. Compare the scope with your agreement, keep the documentation and discuss requirements with the landlord or agent. Ask the cleaning company to stand behind the tasks it agrees to perform, rather than an outcome outside its control.

Should the landlord inspect immediately after cleaning?

Agree the inspection timing with the parties involved. It can help to check the work while there is still time to address missed tasks, but the cleaning company may have its own handover process. Allow for specialist items to dry and confirm any return-visit conditions before booking.

Is move-in cleaning the same package?

It may have similar tasks, but your priorities can differ. A move-in brief might focus on the condition left by previous occupants and readiness for furniture delivery. Explain the actual goal and condition rather than assuming the same package name means the same checklist for every property.
