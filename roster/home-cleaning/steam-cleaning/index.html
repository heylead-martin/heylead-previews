Steam cleaning in Singapore

By Roster editorialUpdated 12 October 20265 min read

Steam cleaning uses hot vapour to loosen dirt and refresh surfaces such as sofas, mattresses, curtains, carpets and hard floors. In Singapore's humid climate it is a popular way to deal with dust and odours in fabric furniture. Check whether your material can take heat and moisture, how long drying takes and what the company's rate actually covers.

What steam cleaning is good for

Steam helps lift dirt that has worked into fabric and textured surfaces, and it can refresh items that are hard to wash, such as a sofa or a mattress. Many companies combine steam with vacuuming, spot treatment or extraction, so the method on the quote matters as much as the word "steam".

Steam is not a cure for every stain. Old marks, dye transfer, water rings and some oily stains may not lift fully, and some may need a different treatment. Ask the company what result it expects on your item and whether it will test a hidden area first.

Check the material first

Heat and moisture suit some materials and damage others. Look for the care label on sofas, cushions and curtains, and share a photo of it with the company. Leather, silk, velvet, some timber finishes and unsealed stone can react badly to steam, and some engineered floors are not designed for it.

Tell the company about antique or expensive items, pieces with glued joints and anything that has been cleaned before with a different method. A careful company will tell you if steam is not the right choice and suggest an alternative, such as dry cleaning or a specialist upholstery treatment. The sofa and carpet cleaning guide covers those alternatives in more detail.

Drying time in a humid climate

Fabric that stays damp for too long can smell musty or grow mould, which is a real concern in Singapore. Ask how much moisture the method leaves behind and how long drying should take. Plan to run a fan or the aircon, open windows where practical and keep people and pets off the item until it is dry.

Book the clean on a day when the room can stay ventilated. If you are cleaning a mattress, have spare bedding ready in case it is not dry by night. Ask the company what to do if the item still feels damp after the expected drying time.

How steam cleaning is priced

Companies price steam cleaning in different ways: per seat for sofas, per piece or size for mattresses, per panel or by weight for curtains, and per area or by session for floors. A low starting rate may only cover the smallest size, a single seat or a minimum order.

The published rates further down this page come from company websites in the Roster directory, one matching rate per company with its source and capture date. Compare them on the same item and size, and check the source for minimum orders, transport charges and GST.

  • What exactly is included in the price: vacuuming, stain treatment, deodorising?
  • Is there a minimum order or a transport charge?
  • Are cushions, armrests or the underside of a mattress charged separately?
  • What happens if a stain does not come out?
  • Is GST included?

Steam cleaning and hygiene claims

Some companies describe steam as sanitising or disinfecting. Hot steam can help reduce some germs and dust mites on surfaces, but results depend on the temperature reaching the surface, the contact time and the material. Treat broad claims with care, and ask the company to explain its method rather than relying on a label.

If someone at home has allergies or asthma, ask about vacuuming with a fine filter, drying time and any chemicals added to the steam. For a deeper clean of the whole home, the deep and spring cleaning guide explains how room-by-room cleans are scoped.

Preparing for the visit

Clear the area around the items and remove loose cushions, toys and small objects. Vacuum or tidy obvious debris if you can, so the cleaner can focus on the treatment. Point out stains and tell the company what caused them if you know, because the cause affects the treatment.

Make sure there is a power point near each item and a place to fill water if the equipment needs it. In a condo, check whether the company needs to register at the guardhouse or book the service lift.

After the clean, inspect the items in good light while the cleaner is still there. Check for remaining stains, water marks and any change in colour or texture. Raise concerns before the team leaves, and keep photos of the before and after condition.

Steam cleaning questions

Is steam cleaning safe for all sofas?

No. Many fabric sofas handle steam well, but leather, velvet, silk and some delicate or glued pieces may be damaged by heat and moisture. Share the care label and fabric with the company and ask it to test a hidden area first.

How long does a steam-cleaned mattress take to dry?

It depends on the method, the mattress and the room. Ask the company how much moisture it leaves behind and how long to wait. Keep the room ventilated and have spare bedding ready in case it is not fully dry by night.

Does steam cleaning kill dust mites?

Hot steam can help reduce dust mites on surfaces, but results depend on temperature, contact time and the material. Ask the company to explain its method and whether it also vacuums with a fine filter.

Can curtains be steam cleaned without taking them down?

Some companies clean curtains in place, while others prefer to take them down. It depends on the fabric, length and condition. Ask which method the company uses and whether shrinkage or water marks are a risk for your curtains.
