Sofa and carpet cleaning in Singapore

Compare sofa and carpet cleaning in Singapore. Check fabric suitability, stain expectations, drying arrangements and how companies price the work.

Sofa and carpet cleaning in Singapore should begin with the material, care instructions and condition of the item. Ask the company to explain its proposed method, expected result and drying arrangements before booking. Compare the full item or area being cleaned, since seat counts and square-metre rates can describe different scopes.

Identify the item before choosing the cleaning method

A useful upholstery enquiry starts with what the item is made from and how it is constructed. Share the manufacturer's care label if available, along with photographs of the whole item and any affected area. Do not assume that two sofas of the same size can use the same process.

Describe removable cushions, fixed upholstery, mixed materials and decorative details. For a rug, include dimensions, backing information and whether it has a label. For fitted carpet, explain the rooms, furniture and access. If the material is unknown, say so rather than guessing.

The home cleaning directory lets you compare companies offering these services. Confirm that the company handles the actual material and item you have before treating a general upholstery listing as a suitable match.

Compare methods by suitability, not the name

The cleaning method should suit the item and its care instructions. Companies may advertise extraction, shampooing, steam or dry methods, but the label alone does not describe every product, machine or step involved. Ask what the proposed process does and why it is appropriate for the material.

A company should be willing to discuss limitations and test an appropriate discreet area when its process requires it. Do not ask for a more aggressive method simply because the first option cannot promise complete stain removal.

Stains, odours and realistic expectations

Stain treatment needs a history as well as a photograph. Tell the company what caused the mark if you know, how long it has been present and what has already been applied. Previous treatments can affect what the next cleaner is willing or able to attempt.

Separate your goals into visible staining, general soiling and odour. Ask which of those the quote addresses and what outcome is realistic. A company may be able to improve appearance without guaranteeing that every mark disappears, especially when material damage is involved.

Avoid treating cleaning as a promised medical or pest-control outcome. If a company makes an allergy, disinfection or dust-mite claim, ask what evidence supports the specific treatment. For an ordinary household booking, material suitability and a clear scope remain useful decision criteria.

How sofa and carpet cleaning prices are quoted

Sofa prices may depend on seats, dimensions, configuration or the whole item. Ask how an L-shaped sofa, chaise, ottoman or extra cushions are counted. A two-seat label is not enough when the item is unusually large or includes separate components.

Carpet and rug quotes may depend on area, item size, condition and whether work happens on-site. Request a total based on measurements and photographs. Clarify collection, delivery, minimum booking, transport and GST where applicable, as well as any separate stain-treatment charge.

A comparison should name the same items and requested result. Do not compare a routine surface clean with a quote that includes additional treatment or off-site handling without noting the difference. The company's written scope should make those differences easy to see.

Plan drying and room use before the appointment

Drying arrangements are part of the booking, not a detail to discover afterwards. Ask the company how long it expects the item to be unavailable under your home's conditions and what ventilation or equipment it recommends. Do not rely on a universal promise that every sofa will be ready in a fixed number of hours.

Describe whether windows can be opened, whether the room is occupied and whether children or pets need access. For mattresses or bedroom carpets, schedule the work so you have an alternative if the area cannot be used immediately. Ask who confirms when normal use can resume.

If a rug is collected, confirm the collection window, tracking or receipt process and expected return arrangement. Photograph the item before it leaves and retain the job details. Make sure the company knows about pre-existing damage or areas that need special handling.

Combine upholstery cleaning with a home clean

Combining services can be convenient when each task has an explicit scope. Ask whether the same team handles upholstery and household cleaning or whether separate specialists attend. A bundled price should still identify the items, methods and exclusions for each part of the job.

Coordinate the sequence with other work. A deep clean or move-out clean may include dust-producing tasks nearby, while an upholstery appointment may leave furniture temporarily unavailable. Agree the order and allow practical access to the rooms.

Check whether furniture movement is included. Heavy items, delicate legs and fitted pieces may need different arrangements. Clear smaller possessions beforehand, but do not dismantle furniture unless the company has specifically agreed how it should be prepared.

Inspect the result and keep the care information

An end-of-job discussion should cover what was done, what remains and how to care for the item afterwards. Ask the cleaner to point out any stain that could not be removed and explain whether further treatment is appropriate. Do not approve repeated work without understanding its purpose and limitations.

Keep the receipt, product or process information provided and any written follow-up terms. If a concern appears after drying, report it through the company's stated process with photographs and the job reference. Different businesses have different reporting windows and return-visit terms.

  • Confirm the complete item list before work starts.
  • Discuss existing damage and treatment limitations in advance.
  • Obtain clear instructions for drying and resuming use.
  • Ask whether protective or conditioning add-ons are optional.
  • Keep collection records for any items cleaned off-site.

Sofa and carpet cleaning questions

Can every fabric sofa be wet-cleaned?

Do not assume so. Provide the care label and ask the company to confirm suitability for the actual material and construction. If there is no label, request an assessment. Choosing a method based only on the appearance of the fabric can overlook details that matter to the cleaning process.

Is steam cleaning the same as extraction?

Companies may use these terms differently in advertising. Ask them to describe the actual equipment, moisture involved and treatment steps in the quote. That explanation is more useful than comparing package names alone, particularly when drying time and material compatibility are important to your booking.

Can a company guarantee removal of a pet odour?

Ask it to assess the item and explain what its treatment can reasonably achieve. The source, duration and materials involved matter. A general promise on a website should not replace a discussion of your specific item, the process proposed and what happens if the expected result is not achieved.

How should I compare carpet dimensions?

Give the company measured dimensions or a clear floor plan and identify the exact areas to be cleaned. Confirm whether stairs, small landings, rugs or furniture-covered areas are counted separately. Ask for the total charge and minimum booking so the unit rate does not hide a different overall cost.
