How these figures are calculated

Prices are averages of the lowest matching advertised rate from each company, rounded to the nearest dollar. We compare one named job and unit at a time, exclude identifiable promotions and annual packages, and require at least three companies. Minimum bookings, GST, parts and call-out fees may differ. These are directory samples, not market-wide averages or quotes.

Company ratings are averaged equally across listings with an eligible rating. Each company's score already combines compatible sources by review count. Unrated companies and incompatible scores are excluded. Counts cover published listings; dots only show current, verified building locations.
