Before you call an aircon technician in Toa Payoh

Count your indoor units and note the brand and model of each, plus whether you need a general service, a chemical wash or a repair. A photo of the unit label and any leak or error code helps the company quote before the visit to Toa Payoh.

Ask whether the price is per unit and whether a minimum number of units applies. The aircon servicing guide explains the common service types.

Ask for the call-out or inspection charge before the visit, and agree the price of any extra work before it starts.
