Before you call a handyman in Sengkang

List every small job you want done in one visit, with photos and measurements, and say which items you are supplying. Give the block, floor and unit so the company can confirm it covers your part of Sengkang.

Ask whether the price is per job, per item or per hour, and whether a minimum charge applies. The handyman guide covers the common jobs.

Ask for the call-out or inspection charge before the visit, and agree the price of any extra work before it starts.
