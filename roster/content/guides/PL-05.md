Water heater installation and repair in Singapore can involve the appliance, plumbing and electrical or gas connections. Identify the heater type and describe the fault before booking. For replacement, compare a complete installed scope and confirm who handles each trade, rather than choosing from the product price alone.

## Which water heater do you have?

A useful water-heater enquiry starts with the existing equipment and the problem you want solved. Share the brand, model and type if they are visible without opening the appliance. Describe whether the issue is no hot water, leaking, unusual operation or an electrical trip.

For a new installation, explain how many outlets you want to serve and how the household uses them. Ask the installer to assess the available space and connections before recommending a model. A product that looks suitable online may need different installation arrangements at your property.

Start with the [plumbing directory](/roster/plumbing/) to compare companies that describe water-heater work. If electricity is part of the fault, also check the [electrical services guide](/roster/electrician/guide/) so the booking includes the right professional.

## Instant, storage and other systems

The heater type affects space, connections and the questions to ask about installation. Instant and storage electric heaters have different arrangements, while gas and other systems need their own suitability assessment. Do not treat these options as interchangeable products that can use every existing connection.

Table: Compare the installation questions for water-heater types

| Type | Main planning question | Ask the installer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Instant electric | Fit with the intended outlet and electrical arrangement | What work is needed beyond the appliance? |
| Electric storage | Space, support and the outlets served | How are mounting and connections assessed? |
| Gas | Suitability of the gas and water installation | Who carries out the licensed gas work? |
| Heat-pump or solar arrangement | Whole-property suitability and system design | What assessment and specialist support are needed? |

Ask for the proposed model and the reason it fits your usage. If you are comparing alternatives, request complete installed quotes for each rather than comparing a basic product listing with a fully specified installation package.

## Repair or replace a faulty heater?

The decision should follow an assessment of the fault, equipment condition and available repair options. Ask the company to explain what failed and whether a repair is appropriate. A heater's age alone does not provide a complete diagnosis or a reliable replacement deadline.

Compare the repair scope, parts availability and warranty with the full cost of replacement. If you replace the unit, include removal, installation materials and any changes to the existing connections. The appliance price on its own does not show the cost of completing the job.

If the heater trips the power or shows signs of an electrical problem, stop using it and arrange qualified assessment. Do not repeatedly reset it or open the casing to investigate. The [urgent electrical guide](/roster/electrician/24-hour/) explains how to prepare an enquiry without attempting electrical troubleshooting.

## Who handles plumbing, electricity and gas?

Assign each part of the installation to the appropriate professional. [EMA's guidance](https://www.ema.gov.sg/consumer-information/electricity/engaging-licensed-workers) covers electrical work by licensed workers. The company should identify who is responsible for the connection and related electrical work in your proposal.

For the water-side scope, check the [PUB plumbing-work requirements](https://www.pub.gov.sg/Professionals/Requirements/Licensed-Plumbers/Plumbing-Works). Gas installations need the relevant licensed gas worker, as described by [EMA](https://www.ema.gov.sg/regulations-licences/licences/worker-licences/gas-service-workers-licence). A business advertising heater installation should explain how the required trades are coordinated.

If different contractors are involved, agree the order of visits and who completes the final handover. Avoid a situation where each assumes the other has checked a connection or supplied a necessary component. Keep the division of responsibility in the written scope.

## What belongs in an installed quotation?

A complete heater quotation identifies the equipment and every agreed part of the installation. Ask whether the company supplies the appliance or installs one you purchase. If you supply it, obtain compatibility confirmation before ordering and clarify how product defects will be handled.

- Exact heater model and the intended outlets or use.
- Removal and disposal of the old unit where relevant.
- Mounting, pipe connections and installation materials.
- Electrical or gas work, with the responsible professional identified.
- Access work and restoration of affected finishes.
- Testing, demonstration, invoice and warranty information.

Ask about GST, transport, visit charges and any work that remains provisional. If the company needs a site assessment to confirm part of the job, establish that assessment fee first. Approve additional work only after receiving an explanation and revised price.

## HDB, condo and access considerations

Property requirements should be checked before a replacement becomes a confirmed installation date. Tell the company whether the home is HDB, condo or landed, and share management instructions or access restrictions. Ask how the equipment and connections fit the actual available space.

For electrical changes in an HDB flat, the [HDB electrical-work guidance](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/managing-my-home/renovation-and-maintenance/renovation/renovation-guidelines/electrical-works) is relevant to the responsible worker's assessment. Do not assume that replacing an appliance means every proposed connection change is automatically outside those requirements.

If cabinetry or a false ceiling limits access, show it in the enquiry. Agree who opens and restores the surrounding area if necessary. For a tenanted home, confirm the owner's approval and who can authorise a different model or extra work on the day.

## Handover and ongoing care

The installer should explain the completed arrangement and the manufacturer's operating instructions at handover. Ask it to demonstrate normal operation and identify the documentation you should retain. Compare the installed model with the quote and keep proof of purchase for warranty purposes.

Follow the maintenance instructions for the actual equipment. Ask the company what inspections or care it recommends and which tasks should be left to a qualified professional. Do not apply a generic maintenance timetable to a different type of heater without checking the manufacturer's guidance.

If a leak or fault appears later, record what happens and contact the responsible company with the job reference. The [leak-repair guide](/roster/plumbing/leak-repair/) can help you describe water-related symptoms. Keep clear of affected electrical areas and arrange qualified help for electrical concerns.

## Water heater questions

### Can a plumber replace an electric water heater alone?

The answer depends on the full scope and the qualifications of the people doing it. Ask who handles the water connections and electrical work, and confirm the appropriately licensed worker where required. A single company may coordinate several trades, but its quote should make those responsibilities clear.

### Can I swap a storage heater for an instant model?

Ask for an assessment of the proposed arrangement before buying equipment. The space, outlets, electrical provision and plumbing may need different work. Compare a complete installation proposal, including any alterations, rather than assuming the new model is a direct substitute because both provide hot water.

### Does a leaking heater always need replacement?

Not necessarily, but the source and condition need to be assessed. Tell the company where water appears and whether the appliance is still operating. A connection issue and a fault in the appliance can have different remedies. Avoid using a leaking unit near electrical components while waiting for qualified help.

### Is installation covered by the product warranty?

Do not assume so. Product warranty and installation workmanship cover can have different providers and conditions. Ask for both sets of terms, who you should contact first and which documents are needed. Keep the purchase invoice, installation record and model details together for a future service enquiry.
