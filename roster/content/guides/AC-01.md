## Which aircon service do you need?

Choose an aircon service by the problem or project, rather than the name of a package. A unit that is due for cleaning needs a different conversation from one that repeatedly trips the power or a new flat that needs its first system. Describe the symptoms before agreeing to a treatment.

For an existing system, note which rooms are affected, when the problem started and whether anything changed recently. For a new installation, start with the rooms you want to cool and how you use them. These details help a company decide whether it can quote remotely or needs a site visit.

## Servicing, cleaning and repairs are different jobs

Routine servicing is a maintenance visit, while repair work addresses an identified fault. A package may include cleaning and checks but exclude replacement parts, fault tracing or extra dismantling. Ask for the written inclusions instead of assuming every task mentioned in an advertisement is included in the starting price.

The same symptom can lead to different recommendations. Poor cooling alone does not tell you whether a unit needs cleaning, a repair or another intervention. Ask the technician to explain the finding, the proposed work and the expected result before approving an additional charge.

[Daikin's maintenance guidance](https://www.daikin.com.sg/business/faqs/) distinguishes servicing from deeper cleaning needs. Follow the guidance for your own equipment and obtain an assessment when a fault persists. Our [servicing guide](/roster/aircon-servicing/guide/) explains how to compare the scope of a maintenance visit.

## Choosing a system for your home

Choose an aircon system around the rooms, installation constraints and daily use in your home. A bedroom used overnight is a different brief from a living area that opens into a kitchen. Tell the installer which rooms will run together, rather than choosing only by the number printed in a package name.

System labels can make two quotations look comparable when the equipment is different. Ask for the indoor and outdoor model numbers, the proposed capacity for each room and the total installed scope. Compare the same configuration before deciding that one quote is cheaper.

Our [guide to aircon types](/roster/aircon/types/) explains the practical differences between split, multi-split, cassette, ducted and portable options. It is a starting point for the discussion, not a substitute for sizing the system at your property.

## What changes between HDB, condo and landed homes?

The building affects installation access, permissible locations and the work that needs arranging. Share your property type early, along with any management instructions. Do not assume a company can move an outdoor unit or change a pipe route simply because the equipment fits in the available space.

For HDB homes, the [air-conditioner installation guidelines](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/managing-my-home/renovation-and-maintenance/renovation/renovation-guidelines/air-conditioner-installation-works) cover approved arrangements, electrical loading and installer requirements. Have the installer check the rules applicable to your flat. Condo residents should also obtain their management's requirements before confirming a date.

In landed homes, explain access to each outdoor unit, the storeys involved and any work already planned. If cleaning or renovation is happening at the same time, agree the order of visits so that completed work is not disturbed unnecessarily.

## Compare the full price, not only the headline

An aircon quote is most useful when you can see what the total buys. The same advertised amount might refer to one indoor unit, a minimum multi-unit booking or a recurring contract. Check the unit of charge, number of visits and payment terms before comparing the figures.

Ask separately about inspection, transport, parts, disposal, extra pipework and GST where applicable. A low starting price is not automatically poor value, and a higher figure is not automatically a more complete service. The written scope explains the difference.

## How to read an aircon company's profile

A useful profile helps you check service fit before you call. Start with the jobs offered, the areas served and any published price conditions. A business address shows where a company is based; it does not establish how far the technicians travel or whether a slot is available.

Read the review count alongside the average rating, then follow the source to understand the comments. Look for experiences relevant to your job, such as installation handover or recurring servicing. ## Prepare a useful enquiry

A short, specific enquiry makes it easier to receive comparable replies. Include the number of indoor units, the brand and model if known, your town, property type and a description of the job. Photos of accessible labels or the affected area can help, but avoid opening equipment to obtain them.

State whether you want maintenance, diagnosis or a full installation proposal. Include preferred dates and any access restrictions. If a tenancy agreement requires a servicing receipt, mention that before booking so you know what documentation the company provides.

- Ask what is included in the quoted amount and what requires separate approval.
- Confirm who will attend, the appointment window and how delays are handled.
- Request written warranty terms for any repair or new installation.
- Keep the final quote, receipt and relevant model information together.

## Aircon questions

### How often should aircon be serviced?

Set the schedule using the equipment manufacturer's guidance, how heavily the units are used and the advice of the company inspecting them. A single interval will not suit every household. Ask what a proposed maintenance plan includes and whether it can be adjusted for rooms used less often.

### Does an aircon package include repairs?

Only if the package says so. Cleaning, inspection, labour and parts can be separate items. Before booking, ask whether a technician can diagnose a fault during the visit and how additional work will be authorised. A service contract should explain its exclusions as clearly as its benefits.

### Can I compare companies before choosing a service?

Yes. Browse the directory and explain the symptoms to shortlisted companies. You do not need to diagnose the equipment yourself. Ask whether the initial visit is for inspection or treatment, what the visit costs and whether that fee is credited towards a repair you subsequently approve.
