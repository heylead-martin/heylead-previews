Aircon servicing in Singapore usually starts with cleaning and checking an existing system. Compare the number of units, the work included and any minimum booking before choosing a company. If the unit has a fault, describe it first so the company can distinguish maintenance from a separate inspection or repair.

## What should an aircon servicing visit include?

An aircon servicing visit should have a written scope that you can understand before booking. Companies use different package names, so ask which indoor components they clean, what checks they perform and whether drainage work is included. The words general service are not a complete specification on their own.

Tell the company how many units need attention and whether all of them are accessible. Mention ceiling-mounted equipment, unusual heights and any unit that cannot be reached without moving furniture. This helps avoid a quote based on an ordinary wall-mounted unit being applied to a different job.

For the wider choice between maintaining and replacing a system, start with the [Singapore aircon guide](/roster/aircon/). For the visit itself, make the scope, total charge and process for extra work the centre of the conversation.

## General servicing or deeper cleaning?

General servicing and deeper cleaning should be compared by what the technician will actually do. A more intensive clean may involve different materials, equipment or dismantling. Ask why it is recommended for your unit and what evidence the technician will show before the work begins.

| Option | What to establish | What not to assume |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General servicing | Cleaning checklist and checks included | That repairs and parts are covered |

| Overhaul | Components removed and reassembly checks | That the term has an identical scope everywhere |
| Fault inspection | Diagnostic fee and approval for repairs | That a service price covers diagnosis |

Read the dedicated [chemical wash listings](/roster/aircon-servicing/chemical-wash/) if that work has been recommended. Compare the description of the procedure, not just the label at the top of a price card.

## What if the aircon is not cold or leaks water?

A recurring fault is a reason to request an assessment, rather than repeatedly buying the same cleaning package. Explain whether one room or the whole system is affected, when the symptom occurs and what previous work has been done. Keep any recent invoice available for the technician.

Ask the company whether the quoted visit covers finding the cause, attempting a repair or cleaning only. Those are different commitments. If additional work is suggested, ask for the finding, the proposed remedy, the extra cost and any warranty in writing before agreeing.

Do not open electrical compartments or dismantle the unit to help with a quotation. If water is reaching electrical fittings, stop using the affected equipment and seek qualified help. The [EMA electrical safety guidance](https://www.ema.gov.sg/consumer-information/electricity/electrical-safety) is the appropriate reference for electrical hazards.

## How much does aircon servicing cost?

The useful comparison is the total for the same number and type of units. A per-unit starting rate can depend on a minimum booking, while a single-unit call-out may have a different total. The [servicing price guide](/roster/aircon/servicing-price/) includes current directory examples and explains those conditions.

Ask whether quoted prices include GST, transport, cleaning materials and any diagnostic fee. If the company offers optional work, request the price before it starts. A written approval process is particularly helpful when someone else will be at home during the visit.

For recurring plans, divide the package into the number of visits and the number of units covered. Check whether missed appointments expire, whether you can transfer the contract after moving and how cancellation works. A package only saves money if its scope and schedule suit your household.

## How do you choose a servicing schedule?

A sensible servicing schedule depends on the equipment and how it is used. Tell the company which rooms run nightly, which are occasional-use rooms and whether the system has a history of problems. Ask for the reason behind the proposed interval rather than accepting a standard plan without discussion.

Use the manufacturer's instructions as the starting point. [Daikin's servicing FAQs](https://www.daikin.com.sg/business/faqs/) illustrate why maintenance and fault assessment are separate considerations. Your own model's guidance matters more than a generic timetable intended for every brand and household.

Keep a simple record of service dates, work performed and symptoms noticed afterwards. That history is useful when changing companies or deciding whether a recurring issue warrants a repair assessment. It also makes a servicing receipt easier to find when a landlord asks for it.

## Prepare your home for the visit

Clear access and a shared understanding of the job help a servicing appointment run smoothly. Move small valuables away from the work area and ask the company how it protects flooring, furniture and walls. Confirm whether it needs water access or a particular place for cleaning equipment.

If you live in a condo, check entry arrangements and any management restrictions before the day. For a rented home, confirm who can approve repairs beyond routine maintenance. A technician should not have to guess whether the person opening the door can authorise additional spending.

- Make the unit list available, including any unit that should not be serviced.
- Point out existing marks, leaks or damage before work begins.
- Ask how the team will demonstrate operation after the service.
- Request an itemised receipt with the service date and work completed.

## Compare aircon companies with a clear brief

Service fit should come before a rating alone. Use the directory to compare published services, prices and areas served, then read reviews about the kind of work you need. Installation reviews may say little about the experience of a recurring cleaning contract.

Check the number and date of reviews as well as the average. Follow the linked source if you want to see recent feedback or a company's response. Roster combines compatible rating sources by review count; it does not treat the result as proof that a company will suit every job.

Send the same brief to each shortlisted company so you can compare the replies. Ask about arrival windows, changes to appointments and how concerns after the service are handled. A clear response can be as useful as an attractive starting price.

## Aircon servicing questions

### Is gas top-up part of servicing?

Do not assume it is included or necessary. Ask the technician to explain any recommendation, the checks performed and the separate price. If the same problem returns after a top-up, discuss diagnosis rather than automatically repeating the work. The [gas top-up listings](/roster/aircon-servicing/gas-top-up/) show company-specific service information.

### Should I book a chemical wash every time?

Choose the treatment after the company has considered the unit's condition and the manufacturer's guidance. Request a plain explanation of why ordinary servicing is insufficient for this visit. You should be able to understand the expected benefit, method and cost before agreeing to a more involved clean.

### Is a maintenance contract better than a one-off visit?

A contract can be convenient if its visit frequency, unit coverage and terms match your needs. Compare the full package with equivalent individual visits, including exclusions. Check expiry dates, rescheduling rules and whether repair labour or replacement parts are included before deciding which arrangement is better value.

### Can I request a specific technician?

Ask the company directly. Roster does not assign technicians or maintain live appointment availability. If continuity matters because of an ongoing fault, explain that when booking and provide the previous job reference. Confirm who will attend rather than relying on a note in an earlier conversation.
