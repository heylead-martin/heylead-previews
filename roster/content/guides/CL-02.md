Deep cleaning in Singapore is a planned, detailed clean beyond routine household upkeep. The exact scope varies by company, so agree a room-by-room checklist, access arrangements and exclusions before booking. For spring cleaning, work backwards from your household deadline and leave time for inspection, drying and any separately booked services.

## Deep cleaning or regular cleaning?

Deep cleaning should be defined by additional tasks and attention, not simply a more impressive package name. Ask which areas receive work beyond a regular visit. The answer might involve detailed kitchen cleaning, bathroom fittings, reachable high surfaces or cupboard interiors, but none should be assumed without confirmation.

A regular booking can still cover your priorities if the home is already maintained and the task list is realistic. A detailed reset may need a different team, more time or specialist equipment. Give the company a description of the condition so its proposal matches the job.

## Build a room-by-room scope

A room-by-room scope makes a deep-clean quote easier to assess. Describe what is included, what needs emptying and what can safely be accessed. A company should know whether cupboards are full, large appliances can be moved and windows need internal cleaning only.

Table: A practical deep-cleaning scope to discuss

| Area | Possible tasks to request | Clarify separately |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kitchen | External surfaces, splashback and accessible grease | Oven, fridge and cupboard interiors |
| Bathrooms | Fittings, screens, tiles and accessible surfaces | Damaged sealant, persistent damp and repairs |
| Living areas | Dusting, floors and reachable fittings | Upholstery, high ceilings and heavy furniture |
| Bedrooms | Surfaces, floors and accessible storage | Wardrobe contents, laundry and mattresses |
| Windows and balconies | Reachable internal surfaces and tracks | Exterior access and specialist work |

Use the table as a conversation starter, not as a promise that every provider includes these tasks. Ask the company to return an edited version showing its actual scope and any extra charges.

## What makes one home take longer than another?

Condition and access can affect the job as much as the advertised floor area. Heavy grease, cluttered rooms, many bathrooms and delicate finishes all deserve mention in the enquiry. Photos give the company a better basis for estimating than the number of bedrooms alone.

Ask how the team is organised and whether the quoted duration describes elapsed time or total labour hours. A larger team working for a shorter visit is not automatically equivalent to a smaller team with more time. The task list remains the most useful basis for comparison.

If the booking has a time limit, agree priorities and the process for approving extra hours. If it is a fixed scope, clarify how unfinished items are handled. Record the exclusions so a disagreement about an unbooked task does not spoil the end-of-visit inspection.

## Mould, damp and damaged finishes

Cleaning visible marks does not establish why they appeared. If staining or damp returns, or a surface is damaged, ask for an assessment of the underlying issue rather than treating another clean as a complete solution. Explain the history to the company before it proposes a method.

Do not ask a cleaner to repair plumbing or electrical defects during a cleaning visit. If there is a suspected water problem, the [leak-repair guide](/roster/plumbing/leak-repair/) explains how to prepare an inspection brief. Keep photographs of the condition before cleaning, especially if another contractor needs to investigate it.

Ask the cleaner what result is realistic for affected surfaces and whether the proposed treatment is compatible with the material. Avoid accepting blanket claims that a package will permanently remove every stain or solve every damp problem without an explanation of the cause.

## Products, disinfection and specialist add-ons

An add-on should have a clear purpose and method. If a company offers disinfection or another specialist treatment, ask what is being treated, which product or process is used and what the manufacturer instructions require. Do not infer a health outcome from a general marketing label.

Tell the company about material restrictions and any household requirements before it arrives. Ask whether rooms must be vacated, what ventilation is needed and when surfaces can be used again. Follow the relevant product and professional instructions rather than combining products yourself.

Sofa, carpet and mattress work may be separately priced and scheduled. The [sofa and carpet guide](/roster/home-cleaning/sofa-carpet-cleaning/) explains the questions about fabric suitability and drying. A whole-home package should say explicitly whether those items are included.

## Plan a spring clean around your deadline

A spring-cleaning plan works best when it starts with the date the home needs to be ready. Check company availability early, then allow room for other jobs and the final inspection. Roster does not have live slot information or evidence that a particular company has last-minute capacity.

If you are also servicing aircon, repairing fittings or rearranging furniture, discuss the sequence with the contractors. Avoid booking final cleaning before work that will create dust or require access through freshly cleaned areas. Upholstery cleaning may also need a period when the furniture is not in use.

For festive gatherings, focus the brief on the rooms and furnishings you will use. For a full reset, allow time to sort possessions before the cleaners arrive. Paying a cleaning team to work around unresolved clutter can make the agreed tasks harder to complete.

## Compare quotes and inspect the finished work

A strong deep-cleaning quote identifies the property, condition, checklist and commercial terms. Ask for the total, GST treatment where applicable, included equipment and the cost of optional tasks. If photos are not enough, ask whether an assessment is needed before a fixed price can be confirmed.

At handover, walk through the checklist with the team or its supervisor. Distinguish missed work from permanent wear or damage that cleaning cannot resolve. Record any agreed return visit, including what it covers and whether there is a charge.

- Share the same room list and photos with each company.
- Identify high-value or sensitive surfaces before work begins.
- Confirm what needs to be emptied, moved or disconnected by someone else.
- Keep the written scope and the company's reporting deadline for concerns.

## Deep cleaning questions

### Is spring cleaning a different service from deep cleaning?

Companies may use the terms for similar packages or different scopes. Ask for the task list rather than relying on the label. A seasonal clean might focus on rooms used for gatherings, while another booking may cover the whole home. The agreed work and exclusions should make the distinction clear.

### Does deep cleaning include the inside of cupboards?

Only if the scope includes it. Ask whether cupboards must be emptied beforehand and who will remove or replace their contents. The same question applies to refrigerators, wardrobes and storage areas. Including these tasks in the enquiry helps the company estimate the time and materials accurately.

### Can cleaners remove every stain?

No result should be assumed before the surface and stain are assessed. Tell the company how long the mark has been present and what products have already been used. Ask for realistic expectations and approval before any treatment that could affect the finish or colour of the material.

### Should I be home for the entire visit?

Agree this with the company. It may need someone for access, scope confirmation and the final inspection, even if you do not stay throughout. Confirm who can approve additional work, how the team can reach you and how keys or entry arrangements will be handled securely.
