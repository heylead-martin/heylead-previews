Our general aircon service is designed for routine maintenance of aircon units that are operating normally. Before work begins, our technician checks the unit condition and explains if troubleshooting, repair or deeper cleaning may be required. Cleaning of reusable air filters Evaporator coil surface cleaning Vacuuming and clearing of the drainage pipe

Answer from the company website

A professional aircon servicing includes cleaning air filters, checking refrigerant gas, washing evaporator coils, clearing drainage pipes, and testing overall system performance. Our technicians ensure your aircon cooling efficiency is restored to optimal condition.
