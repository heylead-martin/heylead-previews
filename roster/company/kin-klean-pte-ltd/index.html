HELPFUL TO KNOW

Questions about Kin & Klean Pte Ltd

How is my price worked out?

Answer from the company website

Published prices are starting rates based on floor area and standard property conditions. Final pricing depends on the property’s condition, furnishings, accessibility, required tasks and estimated cleaning time. We confirm your quote before any work begins.

Company FAQ sourceChecked 10 Oct 2026
What’s included in a clean?

Answer from the company website

Only the tasks confirmed in your booking, following the cleaning checklist for your service. Window and grille cleaning is part of the checklist, and appliance cleaning covers only the microwave, oven and fridge, as stated in our checklists. Specialised work - upholstery, curtains and machine floor cleaning - can be arranged in advance and may incur additional charges.

Company FAQ sourceChecked 10 Oct 2026
Do you guarantee every stain comes out?

Answer from the company website

We work hard on them, but complete removal of stains, discolouration, odours, grease, limescale, mould or long-term buildup cannot be guaranteed. We’ll always tell you honestly what to expect.
