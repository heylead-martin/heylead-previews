    Questions about Go Cleaners Pte Ltd

    What areas of Singapore do you serve?

    Answer from the company website

    We serve all of Singapore (except Sentosa), including HDB, condo, and landed properties. Just send us your address on WhatsApp for confirmation.

    Company FAQ sourceChecked 10 Oct 2026
    Will my cleaner arrive exactly on time?

    Answer from the company website

    Our cleaners rely on public transport, so they may arrive up to 15 minutes earlier or later than the booked slot. If there is an unexpected delay beyond that, we will contact you in advance.

    Company FAQ sourceChecked 10 Oct 2026
    How do I pay for the service?

    Answer from the company website

    We accept PayNow and bank transfer. 50% payment is required upfront upon booking, with the remaining 50% balance due after the service is complete.

    Company FAQ sourceChecked 10 Oct 2026
    How far in advance should I book?

    Answer from the company website

    You can book anytime. We accept bookings around the clock via WhatsApp. Last-minute and evening express bookings are subject to cleaner availability, so the earlier you message us, the wider your choice of slots.

    Company FAQ sourceChecked 10 Oct 2026
    Can I cancel or reschedule my booking?

    Answer from the company website

    Yes. Please WhatsApp us at least 24 hours in advance to cancel or reschedule with no penalty.

    Company FAQ sourceChecked 10 Oct 2026
    Are your cleaners insured and licensed?

    Answer from the company website

    Yes. Go Cleaners is a MOM-licensed household service company (UEN 202207807D). All our cleaners are legally employed full-time with valid work permits.

    Company FAQ sourceChecked 10 Oct 2026
    What time slots are available?

    Answer from the company website

    We operate 8:00am to 10:00pm, Monday to Sunday. Our 2-hour Express service is available from 6:00pm onwards.
