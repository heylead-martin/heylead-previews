Yes, we stand behind our workmanship. Most repairs come with a 30-90 day warranty, depending on the service. If the same issue occurs due to our work, we’ll return and fix it at no additional cost.

How soon can you arrive for a repair?

Answer from the company website

For non-urgent jobs, we typically offer same-day or next-day service. For emergency repairs, our team aims to arrive within 60-90 minutes, depending on traffic and your location.

Do I need to provide tools or materials?

Answer from the company website

No. Our technicians arrive fully equipped with professional tools. For common materials like screws, sealants, or standard pipes, we usually carry stock. If you have specific fixtures (like a chosen faucet or light), you can provide them, or we can purchase them on your behalf for a small fee.
