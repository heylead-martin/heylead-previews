About Fair Price Handyman Services

About the business · Checked 10 Oct 2026

Fair Price Handyman Services offers plumbing, electrical work, carpentry, and painting. The team takes residential jobs across Singapore.

Other services include door and lock services and tiling and flooring. Ask the team which option suits the work you have in mind.

For a tailored quote, share the job details, your location and any useful photos with the team. Ask about labour, materials and any call-out charges before booking.

Questions about Fair Price Handyman Services

Do you provide a warranty for your work?

Answer from the company website

Yes, we stand behind our workmanship. Most repairs come with a 30-90 day warranty, depending on the service. If the same issue occurs due to our work, we’ll return and fix it at no additional cost.

Company FAQ sourceChecked 10 Oct 2026
How soon can you arrive for a repair?

Answer from the company website

For non-urgent jobs, we typically offer same-day or next-day service. For emergency repairs, our team aims to arrive within 60-90 minutes, depending on traffic and your location.

Company FAQ sourceChecked 10 Oct 2026
Do I need to provide tools or materials?

Answer from the company website

No. Our technicians arrive fully equipped with professional tools. For common materials like screws, sealants, or standard pipes, we usually carry stock. If you have specific fixtures (like a chosen faucet or light), you can provide them, or we can purchase them on your behalf for a small fee.

Yes. Fair Price Handyman Services is a registered business in Singapore, and our technicians hold the necessary certifications for electrical and plumbing work as required by local regulations.
