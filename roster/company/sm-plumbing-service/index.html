About the business · Checked 10 Oct 2026

SM Plumbing Service offers drain clearing, leak repairs, toilet plumbing, and water heater services. The team takes residential jobs across Singapore.

Other services include tap and mixer services, pipe replacement, sink plumbing, and floor trap services. Ask the team which option suits the work you have in mind.

For a tailored quote, share the job details, your location and any useful photos with the team. Ask about labour, materials and any call-out charges before booking.

What should I do before the plumber arrives?

Answer from the company website

Turn off the water supply at the main stopcock if there's an active leak. Clear the area around the affected fixture if possible. Take photos of the issue - this helps our technician prepare the right tools and parts.
