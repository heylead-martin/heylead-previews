HELPFUL TO KNOW

Questions about Ace Plumbing Specialist

Can you come the same day?

Answer from the company website

Yes. We're on site in 45 minutes to 1 hour for any job you need done today, emergency or routine. If another day works better, we book a time that suits you. Call or WhatsApp with your postal code and the problem, plus a photo if you can, and we reply within 5 minutes, day or night.

Company FAQ source
What should I do before the plumber arrives?

Answer from the company website

Clear the space we need to work in. Empty the cabinet under the sink, move things off the bathroom floor, and unlock the gate or arrange a lift card if your building asks for one. Photos sent ahead help us load the right parts. If something has been leaking, note when it started and whether it worsens with use, because that detail often saves us a step.

Company FAQ source
Do you charge for a quote?

Answer from the company website

Telling you a likely price over the phone or from a photo costs nothing, and we are happy to do it. Coming out to look properly takes a visit of its own, so an on-site survey starts from $30. If you go ahead with the job, we waive it, and you approve the price of the work before we start.

Company FAQ source
What counts as a plumbing emergency?

Answer from the company website

Anything where water is escaping and you cannot stop it, or where you cannot safely use the flat. A burst pipe, water running down a wall, a toilet overflowing, sewage backing up, or water near electrics all count. A dripping tap or a slow drain does not, though both are worth booking soon. If you are unsure, call and describe it, and we will tell you honestly.

Company FAQ source
Do you guarantee your work?

Answer from the company website

Yes. Our work carries a 30-day workmanship warranty. If a repair or installation we carried out fails within 30 days, we come back and fix it free of charge. For choke clearing, the warranty covers the same blockage coming back because of something we missed. A new choke from fresh grease, wipes or debris is a new job.

Company FAQ source
Are you a licensed plumber?

Answer from the company website

In Singapore that title means a PUB Licensed Plumber, and some of our plumbers hold that licence. Work that needs a PUB Licensed Plumber is carried out by one of them. Licensed work costs more, and we tell you the price before we start. You can also ask for a licensed plumber on any job.

Company FAQ source
Do you offer 24/7 emergency plumbing?

Answer from the company website

Yes. We provide round-the-clock emergency callout across Singapore for burst pipes, major leaks, flooding and serious chokes.

Company FAQ source
Which areas do you cover?

Answer from the company website

We serve homes and businesses islandwide across Singapore, from HDB estates to condos, landed homes, offices, F&B outlets and retail units.

Company FAQ source
How do I book a plumber?

Answer from the company website

Call us, WhatsApp us, or fill in the booking form on our contact page with your address and what is wrong, and we reply within 5 minutes, day or night. A photo of the problem helps us load the right parts before we set off. We confirm a date and time with you directly. For an emergency, calling is fastest.
