ABOUT THE BUSINESS

About V2S SERVICES

Read about the company

About the business · Checked 10 Oct 2026

V2S SERVICES offers plumbing, electrical work, carpentry, and painting. The team takes residential jobs across Singapore.

Other services include drilling and mounting, furniture assembly, door and lock services, curtains and blinds, and tiling and flooring. Ask the team which option suits the work you have in mind.

For a tailored quote, share the job details, your location and any useful photos with the team. Ask about labour, materials and any call-out charges before booking.

Company website excerpts & references

The V2S team works across trades, so one visit can often cover more than one job. When a job needs a specialist or a licensed professional, such as a licensed electrical worker, we tell you upfront and can coordinate with them where required.
