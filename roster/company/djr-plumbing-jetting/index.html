About the business ·

DJR Plumbing & Jetting offers drain clearing, leak repairs, toilet plumbing, and water heater services. The team takes residential jobs across Singapore.

Other services include tap and mixer services, pipe replacement, sink plumbing, floor trap services, and water pump. Ask the team which option suits the work you have in mind.

For a tailored quote, share the job details, your location and any useful photos with the team. Ask about labour, materials and any call-out charges before booking.

Common questions

Are you licensed and insured?

Answer from the company website

Yes. DJR Engineering Pte. Ltd. is a PUB-licensed plumbing company, BCA-registered

Company FAQ source
How fast can you get to me?

Answer from the company website

Our average is around 45 minutes, but it depends on where you are and the traffic. For floods, burst pipes and sewage overflows, call rather than message so we can send the nearest crew and talk you through shutting off the water while you wait.

Company FAQ source
How do you charge?

Answer from the company website

We quote after seeing the problem, and you agree the price before we start. The quote covers call-out and labour. If parts are needed, they're listed separately so you can see what you're paying for.

Company FAQ source
How do I book or get a quote?

Answer from the company website

WhatsApp +65 8988 8777 with a photo or short video of the problem and your address, and we'll reply with a price or a time to come and look. For emergencies, call instead. You can also use the form at the bottom of this page.

Yes. Repairs and installations come with our workmanship warranty in writing, and everything is tested before we hand over.
