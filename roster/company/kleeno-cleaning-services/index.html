Questions about Kleeno Cleaning Services

What should I prepare before the cleaner arrives?

Answer from the company website

Please ensure the premises are accessible with water and electricity available. For general cleaning, a ladder (up to 2.6m) and a toilet brush are recommended. For post-renovation cleaning, please ensure all debris and protective sheets are removed before our team arrives.

Company FAQ sourceChecked 10 Oct 2026
Do I need to provide cleaning equipment?

Answer from the company website

No, our team brings all necessary cleaning equipment and supplies. If you prefer us to use your own vacuum or mop, you may inform us in advance.

Company FAQ sourceChecked 10 Oct 2026
Can I reschedule my appointment?

Answer from the company website

Yes. For general cleaning, please inform us at least 2 days (48 hours) in advance before your scheduled service. Rescheduling with less than 48 hours’ notice will result in forfeiture of the session, and it will be non-refundable. For post-renovation cleaning, please notify us at least 5 days in advance so we can check slot availability, as it is subject to availability.

Company FAQ sourceChecked 10 Oct 2026
What is your cancellation policy?

Answer from the company website

All payments are non-refundable once a booking is confirmed. We do not offer cash or credit refunds for cancellations or change of mind. If you need to reschedule, advance notice is required: - At least 5 days for post-renovation, spring cleaning, and move-in/move-out services; - At least 48 hours for all other cleaning services.

Company FAQ sourceChecked 10 Oct 2026
Is payment required upfront?

Answer from the company website

Yes, all cleaning services require full upfront payment during booking to secure your reservation. Payment is processed securely through Stripe on our platform.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards and debit cards through Stripe, our secure payment gateway. All transactions are encrypted and protected with industry-standard security measures.

What are your business hours?

Answer from the company website

We operate Monday through Sunday from 8am to 6pm GMT+8. Support requests received outside these hours will be addressed the next business day.

What’s included in the price of a Kleeno home cleaning?

Answer from the company website

Every session includes essential cleaning supplies such as detergent, a vacuum and a mop, so you don’t need to prepare your own equipment. We use a fresh set of disposable cleaning cloths for every home, never reused between visits. Our products are eco-friendly and non-toxic, safe for homes with young children and pets.

Company FAQ sourceChecked 10 Oct 2026
How is condo cleaning priced?

Answer from the company website

Condo cleaning is based mainly on floor area rather than bedroom count, because condos vary a lot in size. We assess your unit’s size and give you a price that matches its cleaning requirements.
