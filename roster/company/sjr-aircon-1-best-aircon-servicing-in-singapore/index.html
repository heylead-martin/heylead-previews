HELPFUL TO KNOW

Questions about SJR Aircon

Is SJR a BCA-registered aircon contractor?

Answer from the company website

Yes. SJR Air-Conditioned is a BCA-registered contractor under workhead ME01 (Air-Conditioning, Refrigeration and Ventilation Works), Grade L1. Our technicians are trained to handle all major brands.

Company FAQ source
Do you provide a warranty for aircon servicing or repair?

Answer from the company website

Yes. SJR Air Conditioned provide 30-90 days of service warranty for repairs, gas top-up, or parts replacement.

Company FAQ source
Can you service different brands of commercial aircon systems?

Answer from the company website

Yes. Experienced technicians can handle all major brands including Daikin, Mitsubishi Electric, Panasonic, LG, Fujitsu, Toshiba, and Samsung - covering both VRV/VRF and split systems

Company FAQ source
Is SJR BCA-registered for commercial work?

Answer from the company website

Yes. SJR Air-Conditioned is a BCA-registered contractor (workhead ME01, Grade L1) and our technicians are qualified to service commercial ACMV systems. We comply with Singapore's BCA regulations for commercial building M&E works.
