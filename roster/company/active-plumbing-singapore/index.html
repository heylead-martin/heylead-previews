About Active Plumbing Singapore

About the business · Checked 10 Oct 2026

Active Plumbing Singapore offers drain clearing, leak repairs, toilet plumbing, and water heater services. The team takes residential jobs across Singapore.

Other services include tap and mixer services, pipe replacement, sink plumbing, floor trap services, and water pump. Ask the team which option suits the work you have in mind.
