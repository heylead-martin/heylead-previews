How the combined rating works

Each source's average is multiplied by its review count. The results are added together and divided by the total number of reviews. For example, 200 reviews carry more weight than 20.

Live results will need dated source links, compatible five-point scales and checks for duplicated or mirrored reviews. Sources without a usable count or rating will stay separate. No sample scores are used in search filters or structured data.
