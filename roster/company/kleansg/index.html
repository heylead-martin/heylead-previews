Questions about KleanSG

What happens if the assigned cleaner is unavailable?

Answer from the company website

KleanSG can usually arrange a replacement cleaner if your scheduled cleaner is unavailable. If we are unable to provide a replacement for a missed session, the contract can be prorated based on the affected hours.

Company FAQ source
Does KleanSG provide cleaning supplies and equipment?

Answer from the company website

In general, clients provide the cleaning supplies and equipment for regular office cleaning. If required, KleanSG can provide cleaning equipment and supplies at an additional cost.

Company FAQ source
How do I request a quote and when can cleaning start?

Answer from the company website

You can WhatsApp KleanSG, send us an email or submit the contact form with your office location, office size, preferred cleaning frequency and schedule. For larger or more complex requirements, we may arrange a free site visit before preparing a quotation. The general placement timeline is around 2 to 3 weeks, depending on manpower availability and your cleaning requirements.
