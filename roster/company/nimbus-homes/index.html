Questions about Nimbus Homes

Can I use points to partially offset the price of my booking?

Answer from the company website

Yes, you can use Nimbus Points to partially offset the price of your bookings. First, place your booking through the Nimbus Homes App. Then, go to the 'MyBookings' tab, select the session you wish to partially offset, and press the 'Deduct' button. Enter the amount you wish to pay with Nimbus Points.

What happens to my Nimbus Points if I cancel or reschedule my bookings?

Answer from the company website

The impact on your Nimbus Points depends on when you cancel or reschedule your booking: For Advance Cancellations/Rescheduling (24 hours or more before your session):The holding amount, which may include Nimbus Points, will be fully returned to your Nimbus Wallet. This means if you cancel or reschedule early, your Nimbus Points are securely preserved for future use.
