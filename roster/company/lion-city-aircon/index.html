Lion City Air Conditioner Services publishes a detailed rate card for general servicing, chemical cleaning, overhauls and yearly contracts. The listed prices include GST, making it easier to compare the cost for the number of units in your home.

A few things to help you decide

Worth considering for

A homeowner who wants the chemical-wash price in writing before anyone arrives. The price page lists S$87.20 per unit with GST, and the WhatsApp number is the same line as the phone.

Things to check first

A commercial VRV design-and-build job. Also a weak fit if you want a separate leak-test fee quoted before any gas is added. Gas top-up is named as a service, and the overhaul and the yearly contract include it. The price table does not show a standalone gas price.
