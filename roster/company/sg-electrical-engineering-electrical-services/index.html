About SG Electrical Engineering

About the business · Checked 10 Oct 2026

SG Electrical Engineering offers power trip troubleshooting, rewiring, ceiling fan services, and power point installation. The team takes residential jobs across Singapore.

Other services include distribution board services and water heater point. Ask the team which option suits the work you have in mind.

For a tailored quote, share the job details, your location and any useful photos with the team. Ask about labour, materials and any call-out charges before booking.

We provide reliable and professional electrical installation services.
