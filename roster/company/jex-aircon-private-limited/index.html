About JEX AIRCON PRIVATE LIMITED

About the business · Checked 10 Oct 2026

JEX AIRCON PRIVATE LIMITED offers routine aircon servicing, chemical washes, chemical overhauls, and refrigerant top-ups. The team takes residential jobs across Singapore.

Other services include aircon installation, aircon repairs, maintenance plans, and aircon removal and replacement. Ask the team which option suits the work you have in mind.

Use the service list above to compare available prices and options. Share the details of your job with the team to confirm what is included and get a final quote.

Company website excerpts & references

Our aircon company, Jex Aircon, was founded in 2015 by two Mechanical Engineering graduates who experienced firsthand how slow and overpriced aircon services in Singapore could be. They started with one car, two founders ready to get their hands dirty, and a simple goal, do the job properly and charge a fair price.
