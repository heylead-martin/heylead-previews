Questions about City Cooling Pte Ltd - Aircon Specialist Singapore

Do you offer same-day aircon servicing?

Answer from the company website

Often, yes. Appointments run 7 days a week, and same-day slots are available subject to availability, first come, first served.

Company FAQ sourceChecked 10 Oct 2026
Is there a warranty after servicing?

Answer from the company website

Yes. General servicing comes with a 90-day workmanship warranty, and water leaking is covered for 3 months after a general service or chemical overhaul. Spare parts we replace carry a 3 to 6 month warranty, depending on the part.

All major brands, including Daikin, Mitsubishi Electric, Mitsubishi Heavy Industries (Starmex), Panasonic, LG, Samsung, Fujitsu, Toshiba, Midea and Carrier. See our Mitsubishi Daikin servicing pages.
