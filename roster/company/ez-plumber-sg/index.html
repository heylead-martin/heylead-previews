EZ Plumber SG offers drain clearing, leak repairs, toilet plumbing, and water heater services. The team takes residential jobs across Singapore.

Questions about EZ Plumber SG

How fast can a plumber reach me?

Answer from the company website

For urgent cases we aim to dispatch within the hour and reach most parts of Singapore in about 30 to 60 minutes, depending on traffic and job load.

Do you charge for a quote?

Answer from the company website

We give an indicative price over WhatsApp or phone for free. On site, we confirm the price before starting any work so there are no surprises.

Company FAQ sourceChecked 10 Oct 2026
Is there a warranty on the work?

Answer from the company website

Selected repairs and installations carry a 30 to 90 day workmanship warranty. We tell you which applies before the job starts.

Can you supply the parts and fittings?

Answer from the company website

Yes. We carry common parts on the van and can source taps, heaters and toilet bowls for you, or install a unit you already bought. Your local plumber in Jurong West - serving all of Singapore EZ Plumber SG is based in Jurong West and dispatches plumbers islandwide, from Tampines to Tuas. Save our number - we pick up fast.

Why can the final price differ from the estimate?

Answer from the company website

The estimates are for standard jobs. The type and severity of the problem matter: a simple fixture replacement is a different job from tracing a concealed leak or clearing a severe main-line choke. We explain the cause and confirm the price on site before any work starts, and work only goes ahead once you approve it.
