About Air Connection

About the business ·

Air Connection offers routine aircon servicing, chemical washes, refrigerant top-ups, and aircon installation. The team takes residential jobs across Singapore.

Other services include aircon repairs and aircon removal and replacement. Ask the team which option suits the work you have in mind.

Since 2011, we have served over 30,000 customers and maintained more than 650,000 aircon units across Singapore. With a team of 60 experienced technicians, we handle everything from routine servicing and aircon repair to full system installation for HDB flats, condos, offices, and retail spaces islandwide.
