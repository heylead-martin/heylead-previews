About the business · Checked 10 Oct 2026

KMAC Cleaning offers weekly clean, deep clean, one off clean, and steam cleaning in Singapore. Residential bookings are available. Contact the team to discuss your job and arrange a visit.

Other services include aircon servicing and office cleaning. Ask the team which option suits the work you have in mind.

Company website excerpts & references

KMAC International Pte Ltd says it was founded in 2010 and is a government licensed company providing cleaning for residential and commercial clients.

The general cleaning page says one cleaner is assigned for 3 or 4 hours for general (weekly) cleaning, with no admin fee.

The detailed cleaning page says a 50% deposit is required to confirm a booking.
