Questions about Arshinogor Pte Ltd

How much do handyman services cost?

Answer from the company website

Our pricing varies depending on the type of work required. We offer transparent, competitive rates with no hidden fees. Basic repairs start from $50-$80. For an accurate quote, simply WhatsApp us with the details and photos of the work needed. We'll provide a free quotation within minutes.

Company FAQ source
Do you provide islandwide service in Singapore?

Answer from the company website

Yes! We serve all areas across Singapore - North, South, East, West and Central. This includes HDB flats, condominiums, landed properties, and commercial premises. No matter where you are in Singapore, we can get to you.

Company FAQ source
Can I get same-day service?

Answer from the company website

Yes, same-day service is available subject to technician availability. We recommend contacting us early in the day to increase the chances of same-day scheduling. For urgent matters such as leaking pipes or electrical faults, we prioritize emergency appointments.

Company FAQ source
How do I request a quotation?

Answer from the company website

You can request a quotation in three ways: (1) WhatsApp us at +65 8544 4173 with photos and details of the work, (2) Fill in our online Contact Form on the Contact page, or (3) Call us directly. We'll respond with a free, no-obligation quote as quickly as possible.

Company FAQ source
Do you offer warranties on your work?

Answer from the company website

Yes, we stand behind our work. We offer a workmanship warranty on all our services. If there are any issues with our work within the warranty period, we will return and rectify at no additional charge. Warranty period varies by service type - please ask our team for details when booking.

What payment methods do you accept?

Answer from the company website

We accept cash, PayNow, PayLah!, bank transfer and major credit/debit cards. Payment is typically collected upon completion of the job. For larger projects, we may request a deposit upfront.
