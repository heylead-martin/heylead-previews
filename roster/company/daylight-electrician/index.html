About Daylight Electrician Singapore

Daylight Electrician Singapore handles electrical repairs and installations for homes and commercial properties. Its published services include power-trip troubleshooting, wiring, lighting, ceiling fans and power points.

The company lists a Lower Delta Road office and a west branch at Bukit Batok Crescent. Enquiries are handled by phone or WhatsApp; its contact page lists daily hours from 8am to 10pm. The offices do not accept walk-ins.
