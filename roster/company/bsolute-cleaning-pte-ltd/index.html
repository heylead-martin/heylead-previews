Do I need to provide cleaning equipment and supplies?

Answer from the company website

For regular part-time maid service, customers usually provide basic cleaning tools and supplies. This helps our cleaner use the products you prefer for your home. For one-time cleaning services such as spring cleaning or end-of-tenancy cleaning, we can advise what is needed before the appointment.

How early should I book a cleaning appointment?

Answer from the company website

For regular weekly cleaning, it is best to book early so we can arrange a suitable cleaner and time slot. For spring cleaning or end-of-tenancy cleaning, we recommend booking at least 3 to 7 days in advance , especially during weekends, public holidays, and festive periods.
