Questions about Lito Electrical Service

Do you have EMA Licensed Electrical Worker support?

Answer from the company website

Yes. Lito Electrical Services works with EMA Licensed Electrical Worker support where required for residential and commercial electrical work. EMA Licence No.: 7/077235 . Licence Grade 8 support is available for electrical works above 80A.

Company FAQ sourceChecked 10 Oct 2026
Do you provide emergency electrician services?

Answer from the company website

We provide support for urgent electrical problems such as power trips, no power, burning smell, sparking sockets, faulty DB box, damaged switches and lighting issues. Availability depends on schedule and location.

Company FAQ sourceChecked 10 Oct 2026
Do you provide emergency electrician service?

Answer from the company website

Yes. We provide emergency electrical support for urgent issues such as no power, repeated power trips, burning smell, sparking sockets, burnt switches and faulty DB boxes.
