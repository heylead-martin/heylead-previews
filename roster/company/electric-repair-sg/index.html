About Electric Repair SG

About the business ·

Electric Repair SG offers power trip troubleshooting, rewiring, light installation, and ceiling fan services. The team takes residential jobs across Singapore.

Other services include power point installation, distribution board services, and water heater point. Ask the team which option suits the work you have in mind.

For a tailored quote, share the job details, your location and any useful photos with the team. Ask about labour, materials and any call-out charges before booking.
