About Handyman Service Hero

About the business · Checked 10 Oct 2026

Handyman Service Hero offers plumbing, electrical work, painting, and drilling and mounting in Singapore. Service areas include Central, East, North, West. Contact the team to check coverage for your address.

Other services include furniture assembly, door and lock services, curtains and blinds, and tiling and flooring. Ask the team which option suits the work you have in mind.

Company website excerpts & references

Homes in Singapore have their own habits. HDB flats have service yards, floor traps and bomb shelters that need looking after. Condominiums often have false ceilings, built-in wardrobes and water heaters that are tucked out of sight. Commercial units need quick fixes that do not stop the business for long. Our team works in all three, and we ask what kind of property you are in when you message, so we come prepared.
