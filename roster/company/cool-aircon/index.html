Full pricing notes & conditions

Pricing page, opened 10 Oct 2026. The company says it is not GST-registered, so no GST is added, and the prices apply 7 days a week. One-time general service: 1 unit S$55, 2 units S$60, 3 units S$75, 4 units S$90, 5 units S$105, 6 units S$120. Yearly packages start at 2 units. Chemical wash: 1 unit S$110, 2 units S$210, 3 units S$310, 4 units S$410, 5 units S$510, 6 units S$610, then S$95 for each unit past 6. Wall overhaul for 9,000 or 12,000 BTU: 1 unit S$150, 2 units S$280, 3 or more S$130 each. An 18,000 BTU wall unit is S$180. A 24,000 BTU wall unit is S$240. Cassette and ducted units cost extra. Gas, for single-split and multi-split, by the PSI they measure: under 40 PSI is R22 S$90, R410A S$120, R32 S$130. From 41 to 80 PSI is R22 S$110, R410A S$140, R32 S$150. Above 81 PSI is R22 S$130, R410A S$160, R32 S$170. Multi-split troubleshooting is S$50 to S$80 and waived if the repair or service goes ahead. No call-out fee. CBD parking of S$5 to S$20 may be passed on.
