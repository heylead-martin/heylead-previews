Questions about Cool Aircon

Do you offer same-day aircon service in Singapore?

Answer from the company website

Yes - we offer same-day aircon service across Singapore, 7 days a week, subject to availability. We respond to booking requests within 2-4 hours, and if you message us early in the day we can usually send a technician the same day. Our team is on the road island-wide from 8am to 10pm daily, including weekends and public holidays.

Company FAQ sourceChecked 10 Oct 2026
Can you handle urgent or emergency aircon repair?

Answer from the company website

Yes. For urgent aircon servicing and emergency repairs - a unit that suddenly stopped cooling, is leaking water, or won’t switch on - we prioritise getting a technician to you as fast as possible, often the same day. WhatsApp us with your address and the problem, and we’ll confirm the earliest available slot. There is no call-out surcharge for out-of-the-way locations.

Company FAQ sourceChecked 10 Oct 2026
Why book a servicing package instead of one-off?

Answer from the company website

In Singapore’s climate, most aircon needs a general servicing every 3-4 months - but almost nobody remembers to book it until the unit smells, drips, or stops cooling. A servicing package (also called a maintenance contract) fixes both problems: the visits are scheduled for you across the year, and each visit costs less than booking the same service one-off.

Company FAQ sourceChecked 10 Oct 2026
How are the visits scheduled?

Answer from the company website

We track when your next servicing is due and contact you to arrange each visit at a time that suits you - you don’t need to remember the schedule yourself.

Company FAQ sourceChecked 10 Oct 2026
Does the package include chemical wash or repairs?

Answer from the company website

No - packages cover general servicing visits. If a unit needs a chemical wash or a repair, we quote it separately before any work starts, and regular servicing makes expensive deep cleans much less likely in the first place.

Company FAQ sourceChecked 10 Oct 2026
How much does normal aircon servicing cost?

Answer from the company website

From $55 for a single unit, one-time. Multi-unit bookings cost less per unit - 4 units one-time is $90. Recurring packages start at 2 units (from $115 a year for two units, twice yearly); we are not taking single-unit contracts at the moment.
