Full pricing notes & conditions

Pricing page, opened 10 Oct 2026. The company says it is not GST-registered, so no GST is added, and the prices apply 7 days a week. One-time general service: 1 unit S$55, 2 units S$60, 3 units S$75, 4 units S$90, 5 units S$105, 6 units S$120. Yearly packages start at 2 units.
