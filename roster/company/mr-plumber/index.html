MEET THE BUSINESS

About Mr Plumber Singapore

Mr Plumber Singapore lists plumbing repairs and installations for homes and businesses. Its service range covers choked sinks and toilets, leaking pipes, water heaters and replacement fittings.

The company publishes 24-hour availability, seven days a week. It takes enquiries by phone and WhatsApp, with a main office on Lower Delta Road and a separate Paya Lebar desk. There is no showroom or walk-in service.
