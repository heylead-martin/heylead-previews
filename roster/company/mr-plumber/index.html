About Mr Plumber Singapore

About the business · Checked 10 Oct 2026

Mr Plumber Singapore lists plumbing repairs and installations for homes and businesses. Its service range covers choked sinks and toilets, leaking pipes, water heaters and replacement fittings.

The company publishes 24-hour availability, seven days a week. It takes enquiries by phone and WhatsApp, with a main office on Lower Delta Road and a separate Paya Lebar desk. There is no showroom or walk-in service.

How the combined rating works

Each source's average is multiplied by its review count. The results are added together and divided by the total number of reviews. For example, 200 reviews carry more weight than 20.

Live results will need dated source links, compatible five-point scales and checks for duplicated or mirrored reviews. Sources without a usable count or rating will stay separate. No sample scores are used in search filters or structured data.
