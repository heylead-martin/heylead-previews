What does an aircon service include?

Does aircon servicing include a warranty?

Answer from the company website

Yes. Letscool Aircon provides a service warranty for our aircon servicing work. This covers workmanship-related issues within the warranty period, giving customers peace of mind. Warranty terms may vary depending on the service type, and our team will inform you before servicing begins.

Company FAQ sourceChecked 10 Oct 2026
How Much Does It Cost to Fix Aircon Water Leakage?

Answer from the company website

Fixing aircon water leakage in Singapore usually costs between $50 to $90 , depending on the cause. Common reasons include clogged drainage pipes, dirty fan coils, or improper installation. LetsCool Aircon technicians will first diagnose the issue before recommending the right solution.

Company FAQ sourceChecked 10 Oct 2026
Does general aircon service include gas top-up?

Answer from the company website

General aircon servicing does not include refrigerant gas top-up . Gas top-up is only required if there is a refrigerant leak. Our technicians will check the PSI pressure during servicing and will advise honestly if gas refilling (R32 or R410A) is needed.

Company FAQ sourceChecked 10 Oct 2026
Is there a warranty after aircon servicing?

Answer from the company website

Yes. LetsCool Aircon provides a 30-day workmanship warranty after every general aircon service. This gives customers peace of mind and assurance of service quality.
