# Roster competitor gaps, 15 Oct 2026

Scope: who ranks for the main SG keywords, what their pages contain, and what Roster lacks. The rule throughout is sourced data only.

Method: web searches for `aircon servicing singapore`, `plumber singapore`, `electrician singapore`, `handyman singapore`, `part time cleaner singapore`, `aircon chemical wash price singapore` and `home cleaning services singapore price per hour`, plus a few follow-ups. Robots.txt was checked before every fetch, with one request per host per pass and no logins.

- **Not fetched:** Seth Lui and MoneySmart are behind Cloudflare challenges. Urban Company's robots.txt prohibits crawling without written permission. For these three, only search-result snippets were used.
- **SingSaver:** the electrician article redirected to a blog hub, so it is also snippet-only.
- **Results are US-region search results**, not SG SERPs. Treat the rankings as indicative.
- **Not found:** Recommend.sg did not appear for any query. Kaodim shut down on 30 Jun 2022 ([CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/kaodim), [Vulcan Post](https://vulcanpost.com/?p=790227)).

There are two models. Booking platforms (Helpling, Urban Company) put prices, a booking flow and guarantees on service pages. Editorial listicles (MoneySmart, SingSaver, Seth Lui, Honeycombers, Women's Weekly, Homejourney) publish "best N companies" lists with a summary table and FAQs. No independent multi-trade **directory** with per-company profiles ranks. That is Roster's opening.

## What Roster already matches or beats

Roster has:
- per-company profiles with sourced prices, dated multi-source ratings and stated service areas
- per-service pages (e.g. `aircon/servicing-price`, `aircon-servicing/chemical-wash`, `plumbing/choked-drain`, `home-cleaning/part-time-cleaner`)
- 20+ guides
- FAQ, Breadcrumb, LocalBusiness/Plumber/Electrician/HVACBusiness and Service schema
- directory price samples with source lists

No competitor shows per-price sources, capture dates or rating provenance. Helpling's own pages contradict themselves on price and rating, and the listicles go stale. Provenance is Roster's differentiator and should be visible on every gap filled below.

## Recommended order

1. Aircon price matrix (#1) and area pages (#2): highest search demand, built from data already held.
2. Licence panel (#3), quick-picks/summary table (#4) and surcharges table (#6): trust signals and listicle SERP format.
3. A collection pass for hours, warranty and brands (#5, #7, #13) in the next release, each with source URL and capture date.
4. Editorial pieces (#8, #12, #14) and the calculator (#10) once #1 has enough coverage.
