A few things worth knowing

Can a company buy its position?

No. Directory listings are unpaid. Recommended ordering considers the available evidence; you can also sort by average rating, review count or name.

Where do the ratings and reviews come from?

Ratings and reviews link to external sources with capture dates. Compatible scores are weighted by review count. They are not verified Roster bookings.
