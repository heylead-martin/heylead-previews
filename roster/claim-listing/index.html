HOW IT WILL WORK

A few details. A clear next step.

  1. 01

    Find your business

    Enter your business name and tell us your name and role. Owners and authorised representatives can request access.

  2. 02

    Confirm your connection

    Provide a business email and website. Verification would use an official email address or a callback to a published business number.

  3. 03

    Make it your own

    Once approved, you would be able to request updates to your logo, introduction, services, prices, hours and contact details.

BEFORE YOU START

What you would need

  • The business name and its Roster listing
  • Your name and relationship to the business
  • A work email and the company website
  • Access to an official email or published business phone for verification
  • A UEN, if the business has one, and the details you want to update

Documents are not needed for this preview. Any further ownership evidence would be requested privately during a real review.

Will claiming affect reviews or ranking?

A claim would confirm who can manage business details. It would not remove independent reviews or buy a higher position in the directory.

Can I write in first person?

Yes. An approved owner introduction could use "we" and "our" and be labelled as written by the business. Until then, Roster's summaries use third person.
