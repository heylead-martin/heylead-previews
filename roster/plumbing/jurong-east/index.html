Before you call a plumber in Jurong East

Share the block number, the floor and the room with the fault, plus a photo of the pipe, trap or fitting. Say whether it is an HDB flat, a condo or a landed home, and mention any lift booking or parking arrangements the technician needs.

Leaks and chokes in shared areas, such as a common drainage stack or a corridor pipe, are normally handled by your Town Council or management office rather than a private plumber. Check with them first if water is coming from outside your unit, then book a plumber for work inside your home in Jurong East.
