How this list is made
  • Who qualifies: companies on Roster whose main trade is plumbing, with a Google Maps rating recorded with its review count, link and check date. 53 of 100 qualify.
  • Minimum: 20 Google reviews.

Questions about this ranking

How is this list ranked?

By Google Maps rating and review count only. Each company's score is (reviews × rating + 50 × 4.5) ÷ (reviews + 50), so small review counts are pulled toward 4.5. A company needs at least 20 Google reviews to qualify.

Why is a 5.0 rating sometimes ranked below a 4.9?

A 5.0 from a few dozen reviews says less than a 4.9 from thousands. With these settings, 5.0 from 20 reviews scores 4.64 and 4.8 from 900 reviews scores 4.78, so the larger record ranks higher.

Can a company pay to be on this list?

No. Roster listings are unpaid, nobody can pay for a position, and Roster does not move companies up or down by hand.

Which ratings are not used?

Only the Google Maps rating counts. Ratings on Trustpilot, Carousell, Yelp, Facebook or a company's own website are not used, and companies with fewer than 20 Google reviews or no dated Google rating are left out.

Is this a recommendation?

No. It is a ranking of public Google Maps ratings as Roster recorded them, not a recommendation or a quote. Ratings change, so check the current rating and ask for a written quote before you book.
