BEFORE YOU BOOK

Your guide to plumbing

By Roster editorialUpdated 12 October 20266 min read

Plumbing services in Singapore cover choked drains, toilet faults, water leaks, fittings and water heaters. Describe the problem before choosing a repair, and compare the inspection fee, proposed scope and final quotation. For regulated plumbing work, check the licensed person responsible instead of relying only on a business name or review score.

Which plumbing service fits the problem?

The first step is identifying where the problem appears and what happens when you use the fixture. A slow sink, a constantly running cistern and a ceiling stain need different enquiries. You do not need to diagnose the cause yourself to provide useful information.

Describe when the problem began, whether it is getting worse and which fixtures are affected. Include previous repairs and any work recently carried out nearby. Clear photos of accessible areas can help a company decide whether it can estimate the job or needs an inspection first.

What if the plumbing problem is urgent?

An urgent enquiry should explain the immediate situation before discussing a standard package. Tell the company whether water is still escaping, a fixture cannot be used or several outlets are affected. Confirm that someone is actually available to attend and ask for the arrival estimate directly.

Do not assume a business advertising long hours has a technician free now. Ask whether the person replying is confirming dispatch or only collecting details. Establish the call-out or inspection charge, any after-hours premium and what happens if you decide not to proceed with a repair.

Keep away from water affecting electrical fittings and seek appropriate help. A plumbing enquiry is not a substitute for an emergency response to an immediate danger. Avoid dismantling equipment or attempting a risky repair while waiting for a quotation.

When do you need a PUB Licensed Plumber?

The scope determines the licensing requirement. PUB's plumbing-work guidance distinguishes regulated work requiring a Licensed Plumber from specified simple tasks, including clearing blockages. Ask who will undertake your particular work and check the official listing when a licence is required.

A company name is not the same thing as the name of the licensed person attending or responsible for the job. Request that information in the booking discussion. If the work expands after inspection, revisit the scope and the qualifications needed rather than assuming the original arrangement covers everything.

Roster profiles preserve sourced company information. They are not a blanket certification of licences, insurance or workmanship. A statement on a company website should be checked against the relevant official source when it affects the job you are booking.

What should a plumbing quote include?

A plumbing quote should separate finding the problem from fixing it. Ask whether the inspection or call-out fee is payable even if no repair goes ahead, and whether it is credited against approved work. That makes the first appointment easier to compare across companies.

For the repair, request labour, parts, access work and any making good as separate items where useful. Ask about disposal, transport and GST where applicable. A quote for replacing a fitting may not include restoring cabinetry, tiles or paint disturbed to reach it.

Published prices on Roster are company-specific examples with conditions. A starting rate for one type of choke or fitting does not establish the cost of every plumbing job. Confirm the actual scope and approve additions before the technician proceeds.

Who should arrange a shared-building repair?

The location and cause of a problem affect who needs to be involved. If several homes or common areas are affected, contact the relevant building management instead of assuming it is an isolated fixture repair. Keep photos and notes so everyone is discussing the same symptoms.

For HDB ceiling leaks, HDB's guidance explains coordination with the upper-floor neighbour; top-floor roof leaks are a Town Council matter. Check the applicable process before agreeing to extensive work.

Condo residents should report potential shared-system problems to their management and establish the next step for the actual building. A private plumber's initial inspection may help describe the issue, but it does not by itself determine responsibility between owners and management.

Water heaters can involve more than plumbing

A water-heater job needs a brief covering the appliance and its connections. Replacing a heater, repairing a water connection and investigating a power trip are different scopes. Tell the company what type of heater you have and whether the fault involves water, heat or electricity.

Ask who handles each part of the job, including electrical or gas work where relevant. Do not assume a plumbing package includes every specialist needed. The water-heater guide explains how to compare the complete proposal.

If the fault involves power, use the electrician directory to find the appropriate help. A clear allocation of responsibility is particularly useful when more than one contractor needs to attend or when replacement equipment must be selected before installation.

Compare plumbers on the details that matter

A strong shortlist combines service fit, clear pricing and relevant review context. Start with companies that describe the work you need and confirm coverage of your area. An office address near your home does not guarantee availability or a lower call-out charge.

Read reviews about similar repairs and look at the count, source and date alongside the average rating. Check how the company communicates about extra work and follow-up. A large review total is useful context, but it is not a substitute for understanding the proposed job.

  • Send each company the same description and photos.
  • Confirm the first-visit charge and any after-hours terms.
  • Ask who approves additional work and how the price is recorded.
  • Obtain an itemised receipt and written repair warranty where offered.
  • Keep the contact details for reporting a recurring problem.

Plumbing questions

Does every plumbing task require a licensed plumber?

No. PUB distinguishes regulated plumbing work from specified simple tasks. The important step is checking the scope of your actual job, especially if an inspection leads to replacement or alteration work. Ask the company to identify the responsible person and verify the relevant licence when the work requires one.

Can I get an exact price from a photograph?

Sometimes a company can quote a straightforward, clearly identified task from photos, but concealed problems may need inspection. Ask which amount is confirmed and which is provisional. Establish the inspection fee and approval process before the visit so uncertainty about the repair does not become uncertainty about every charge.

Are plumbing warranties the same across companies?

No. Ask what the warranty covers, how long it applies and which conditions or parts are excluded. A return of the same symptom may need investigation before the company determines whether it relates to the original work. Keep the invoice and written terms so the discussion is specific.

Can Roster book an emergency plumber for me?

Roster currently helps you compare and contact companies directly. It does not dispatch technicians or confirm live availability. Call the company to explain the urgency and obtain an appointment confirmation. The quote-request experience is a beta preview, so completing it does not send the job to plumbers.
