Before you call a plumber in Bukit Merah

Share the block number, the floor and the room with the fault, plus a photo of the pipe, trap or fitting. Say whether it is an HDB flat, a condo or a landed home, and mention any lift booking or parking arrangements the technician needs.

Leaks and chokes in shared areas, such as a common drainage stack or a corridor pipe, are normally handled by your Town Council or management office rather than a private plumber. Check with them first if water is coming from outside your unit, then book a plumber for work inside your home in Bukit Merah.

The leak repair guide and choked drain guide explain what to describe for the most common jobs.

Ask for the call-out or inspection charge before the visit, and agree the price of any extra work before it starts.
