Home rewiring in Singapore

Plan home rewiring in Singapore. Compare partial and full work, surface and concealed routes, testing, access requirements and itemised quotations.

Home rewiring in Singapore is a planned electrical project based on the condition of the existing installation and the household's needs. Ask a licensed electrical worker to assess the scope, then compare routes, point schedules, access, testing and making good. A room-count package alone does not describe a complete rewiring job.

When should you ask for a wiring assessment?

Ask for an assessment when you have concerns about the installation or are planning changes that need one. Repeated faults, visible damage, uncertain renovation history and a major change in appliance use are useful information for the worker. They are reasons to investigate, not a remote diagnosis that the whole home must be rewired.

If there is an immediate safety concern, deal with that first through the appropriate qualified or emergency help. Do not continue using affected equipment while waiting for a non-urgent renovation quote. The urgent electrical guide explains how to describe the situation safely.

For planned work, start with the electrician directory and request a site-specific assessment. Ask for the findings behind a recommendation so you can understand the proposed extent of the project.

Partial work or full rewiring?

The scope should explain which parts of the installation will change and which remain. A partial project may address a defined area or planned addition, while a full rewiring proposal should still specify its boundaries. Ask how retained sections will be considered in the completed arrangement.

Do not compare a partial quote with a whole-home quote as if the lower amount necessarily represents better value. List the rooms, points, circuits and associated work in each proposal. Ask the worker to explain any limitations or additional work that may become necessary after access is available.

A written scope should also distinguish the electrical work from renovation finishes. Removing cabinetry, opening ceilings and repainting may belong to different contractors. Confirm who coordinates those tasks before the project begins.

Surface routes and concealed routes

Wiring routes should be compared for suitability, access and the effect on the property. Surface trunking and concealed arrangements can have different visual and construction implications. Ask which options are permitted and practical for the actual building rather than choosing solely from photographs.

For HDB flats, consult the electrical-work guidance with the responsible worker. It places restrictions on embedding wiring. A proposal must fit the applicable requirements, not a generic preference for hidden cables.

Build a room-by-room point schedule

A point schedule makes the proposed installation concrete. Mark lights, switches, sockets and equipment connections on a plan, then discuss the intended use with the worker. Furniture placement and room use should be part of that conversation before positions are fixed.

Include the specifications of larger appliances and any planned aircon or water-heater change. Ask the worker to assess the appropriate arrangement rather than assuming every new appliance can share the existing provision. Do not specify electrical components yourself as a substitute for that assessment.

Keep versions of the plan so agreed changes are visible. If a room layout changes during renovation, update the schedule before the electrical team proceeds. Clear coordination reduces the risk of a finished socket being hidden behind cabinetry or a switch ending up in an awkward position.

What affects a rewiring quotation?

A rewiring quote depends on the defined installation and the work needed to reach it. Point quantities, routes, access, products and the existing condition can all affect the proposal. Ask for an itemised scope rather than relying on a single amount tied to the number of bedrooms.

Clarify whether the price includes the distribution-board work proposed, fittings, wiring, testing and documentation. Separate optional products from required installation work. Check removal, disposal, access equipment, GST and finishing costs where applicable.

If the company cannot confirm a concealed condition before opening an area, agree how that uncertainty is handled. Request a clear approval process for additional work. A provisional item should describe the unresolved issue rather than acting as an open-ended permission to increase the bill.

Plan disruption and coordination

A rewiring schedule should explain the sequence and the areas affected, including interruptions to power. Ask whether the property can remain occupied during the proposed work and what arrangements would be needed. The answer depends on the actual scope, so do not rely on a universal completion time.

Coordinate the electrician with renovation, aircon installation and other trades. The aircon installation guide and water-heater guide can help you prepare the equipment details those trades need to share.

For condos, obtain management requirements for access and work before confirming dates. For HDB homes, ask who handles the applicable requirements. In every property, identify who restores finishes and when final cleaning can take place after the relevant work is complete.

Testing, documentation and handover

The completed installation needs the appropriate professional checks and a clear handover. EMA's licensed-worker guidance is the reference for who should carry out or supervise electrical work. Ask the responsible worker what testing and documentation apply to your project.

At handover, compare the completed point schedule with the agreed plan and ask for an explanation of the changes. Keep the records provided, product details and written warranty terms. Ask how to report a concern and which company is responsible if several contractors were involved.

  • Retain the final plan and approved variations.
  • Record any items that remain outstanding and the return date.
  • Ask for the relevant test or submission records for the scope.
  • Keep product warranties separate from workmanship terms.
  • Do not treat visual neatness as a substitute for professional checks.

Rewiring questions

Is there a fixed age when all wiring must be replaced?

Do not use age alone to decide the scope. Ask a licensed worker to assess the actual installation, its history and the household's planned use. A recommendation should explain the findings and the proposed work. This guide does not set a universal replacement interval for every Singapore home.

Can rewiring be done while I live at home?

Ask the company to plan around the specific scope and explain the disruption. Access, temporary loss of power, open work areas and coordination with other trades all matter. Obtain a realistic schedule and agree any occupancy arrangements before booking rather than assuming every project can be completed around normal household use.

Does a rewiring package include new switches and lights?

Only if the quotation says so. A package may include certain fittings while leaving decorative lights, fans or appliances to you. Ask for model or allowance details and clarify supply, installation and warranty responsibilities. Compare the actual included products and labour across quotes, not the package name alone.

Can I choose a concealed route in an HDB flat?

Discuss permitted routes with the responsible worker and check HDB's current conditions. Do not assume all forms of concealment are allowed. If the desired appearance is not compatible with the rules or existing structure, ask for a compliant alternative and agree how it will look before work starts.
