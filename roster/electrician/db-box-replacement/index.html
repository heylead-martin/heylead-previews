DB box replacement in Singapore

Replace or upgrade a DB box in Singapore. Learn the warning signs, repair versus replacement, licensed work and published electrician rates.

DB box replacement in Singapore means replacing your home's distribution board, the panel of breakers and protective switches that controls every circuit. It is usually considered when the board is old, damaged, keeps tripping or cannot handle new circuits, or during a renovation or rewiring. This is licensed electrical work, so ask who the licensed electrical worker is and what testing is included.

What the DB box does

The distribution board, usually called the DB box, takes the incoming supply and splits it into circuits for lights, sockets and high-power appliances. Each circuit has a breaker that trips if it is overloaded or shorted, and a residual current device, often called an ELCB or RCCB, cuts the power if it detects current leaking to earth.

Because every circuit runs through it, the DB box is central to your home's electrical safety. Problems here can affect the whole home, so any work on it must be done by a licensed electrical worker. EMA's guidance on engaging licensed workers explains how to engage one, and the licensed electrician guide explains what to ask.

Signs your DB box may need attention

Some signs point to the board itself, while others point to a circuit or appliance. An electrician should test before recommending replacement.

If a switch trips, the power trip guide explains what to check safely. If you see scorching, smell burning or hear buzzing, switch off the power at the main switch if it is safe to do so and call an electrician. Do not open the DB box cover.

Repair, upgrade or replace?

Not every problem needs a new board. A faulty breaker or residual current device can sometimes be replaced on its own. An older board may be upgraded with modern protection, or replaced entirely if it is damaged, outdated or too small for the circuits you need.

Ask the electrician to explain what they found, what they recommend and why. Compare the cost of replacing individual components with replacing the whole board, and ask whether a full replacement is needed for your plans, such as adding an aircon or water heater circuit.

Renovation and rewiring

DB box replacement is often done with rewiring or a renovation, when new circuits are added and old wiring is replaced. Plan the board's capacity with the electrical layout, so there are enough circuits for the kitchen, bathrooms, aircon and any future needs. The home rewiring guide explains how rewiring is scoped.

In HDB flats, electrical works follow HDB's renovation guidelines. The HDB guidance on electrical works explains the requirements, and the HDB electrical work guide covers planning. Condo residents should check with the management office.

What the job involves

Replacing a DB box usually means switching off the supply, removing the old board, fitting the new enclosure and components, reconnecting each circuit and testing the installation. The power will be off for part of the job, so plan around it and tell household members.

Ask the electrician what testing they carry out after the replacement and what documentation you will receive. Keep the invoice, the name of the licensed worker and any test results with your home records.

How DB box replacement is priced

Electricians usually price DB box work per job, with different rates for replacing a single component, upgrading protection and replacing the whole board. The number of circuits, the components chosen and any rewiring affect the price.

The published rates further down this page come from company websites in the Roster directory, one matching rate per company with its source and capture date. Check each source for what is included, such as the enclosure, breakers and residual current protection.

  • Is the price for a component, an upgrade or a full board?
  • Which components are included, and how many circuits?
  • Is testing included, and what documentation is provided?
  • How long will the power be off?
  • Is GST included?

Labelling and future changes

A well-labelled board makes it much easier to switch off the right circuit in an emergency or before future work. Ask the electrician to label each breaker clearly with the room or appliance it serves, and to leave space for future circuits if you plan to add an aircon, water heater or EV charger later.

Preparing for the visit

Make sure the electrician can reach the DB box easily, and clear any items stored around it. Note which circuits serve which rooms if you know, and list any existing problems, such as breakers that trip. Plan for the power to be off, and switch off sensitive electronics beforehand.

After the job, check that every circuit works, that breakers are labelled and that the board cover is secure. Ask the electrician to show you how to reset a breaker safely.

DB box questions

How do I know if my DB box needs replacing?

Frequent trips, scorch marks, a burning smell, buzzing, an outdated board without residual current protection or no room for new circuits can all be reasons. An electrician should test before recommending replacement.

Can a DB box be repaired instead of replaced?

Sometimes. A faulty breaker or residual current device can be replaced on its own. A damaged or outdated board may need full replacement. Ask the electrician to explain the options.

Does DB box replacement need a licensed electrician?

Yes. Work on the distribution board is electrical installation work and must be done by a licensed electrical worker. Check EMA's guidance and ask the company who will carry it out.

How long will the power be off?

It depends on the job. A component swap is quicker than a full board replacement with new circuits. Ask the electrician for an estimate and plan around it.
