FAQ

Questions Singapore owners ask

Anything not covered here, WhatsApp Martin.

Do you manage Google Ads for Singapore only, or also Bing?

Google Ads is the default. Microsoft Advertising (Bing) is added when desktop B2B or incremental cheap volume is real. We do not clone a messy Google account into Bing.

What media budget do we need?

If daily budget cannot buy enough clicks to learn, we will say so in the review. Many Singapore service businesses need a few thousand SGD a month in media before the account is worth managing. The audit is the honest filter.

Will the account be in HeyLead's MCC only?

We can link an MCC for management. The client account stays yours. You keep history if we ever part ways.

Can you fix a suspended Google Ads account?

Sometimes. Ketch Locksmiths in Singapore is an example of compliance work plus a longer SEO plan after a suspension. There is no guarantee Google will reinstate anyone. We will tell you if the case is realistic.

Do you write the ads in Singlish?

No. Clear professional English converts for commercial search here. Local proof, locations, and offers belong in the copy. Gimmick dialect does not.

How fast is the free SEM review?

About 48 hours for a human review of structure, waste, tracking, and landing-page fit. WhatsApp replies are usually about a minute during working hours, email about 30 minutes.
