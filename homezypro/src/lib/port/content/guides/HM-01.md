Handyman services in Singapore can help with furniture assembly, mounting and a range of small household repairs. Start with a clear job list and confirm what the company actually offers. Electrical, regulated plumbing, gas and window work need the appropriate specialist or licensed worker rather than a general handyman label.

## Which jobs suit a handyman enquiry?

A handyman enquiry works well when each task is specific. A loose cabinet hinge, a flat-pack wardrobe and a shelf installation are different jobs even if all are small. List the item, location, condition and desired result so the company can confirm whether it handles the work.

Do not assume that every business advertising general home services covers every trade. Some focus on assembly and mounting, while others coordinate several specialists. Ask who will attend and how any specialist part of the job is arranged.

Table: Turn a household task list into a useful enquiry

| Task | Details to share | Confirm in the quote |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Furniture assembly | Product model and package status | Assembly, positioning and packaging disposal |
| Shelves or mounting | Item size, intended load and wall details | Assessment, fixings and finishing |
| Curtain rails or blinds | Product, dimensions and location | Supply or installation-only scope |
| Door adjustment | Symptoms, material and hardware photos | Adjustment versus replacement parts |
| Small fitting repairs | Whole-item and detail photos | Parts, labour and follow-up |

For door and window issues, read the [repair guide](/handyman/door-window-repair/) before deciding whether the job belongs with a handyman or a specialist contractor.

## When do you need a different professional?

The task determines the professional needed. [EMA's electrical guidance](https://www.ema.gov.sg/consumer-information/electricity/engaging-licensed-workers) covers licensed electrical work, while [PUB](https://www.pub.gov.sg/Professionals/Requirements/Licensed-Plumbers/Plumbing-Works) distinguishes regulated plumbing from specified simple tasks. Ask about the actual scope rather than relying on the business category.

Gas work and window work also have specialist requirements. For HDB window work, consult the [HDB guidance](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/managing-my-home/renovation-and-maintenance/renovation/renovation-guidelines/window-works) and confirm the appropriate approved contractor. A general repair enquiry should not become unqualified work on a safety-critical installation.

If a task changes after inspection, revisit who is responsible before proceeding. A small visible problem can lead to a broader recommendation, and the original booking may not cover the new scope. Ask for the reason, price and appropriate worker to be identified.

## How do handyman prices work?

Handyman charges may be per task, per visit or based on time, with minimums or separate materials. Ask for the total for your list and clarify what each line includes. A starting mounting price may not cover the bracket, unusual wall conditions or work needed to conceal cables.

For assembly, provide the exact product model rather than describing it only as a wardrobe or desk. Product complexity, access and the number of pieces can change the quote. If packages are incomplete or damaged, ask how waiting time or a return visit is charged.

Compare transport, materials, disposal and GST where applicable. A written quote should explain how additional work is approved. The goal is a clear total for the agreed tasks, not simply the lowest headline rate attached to a broad service category.

## Bundle small jobs without losing the detail

Bundling tasks can make a visit convenient, but the company still needs an itemised list. Group work by room and attach photos so it can estimate the tools, materials and time. Ask whether the combined quote has a visit minimum or a different basis from individual jobs.

Put the most important tasks first if the visit is time-limited. Identify any item that requires a part to be sourced in advance. A company arriving without the right hinge or bracket may be unable to finish that task regardless of how much time was booked.

Avoid adding several unrelated jobs on the day without discussing the effect on time and price. Ask for a revised total before the extra work starts. Clear scope protects the appointment from becoming an open-ended list that neither side priced accurately.

## Mounting, drilling and product compatibility

Mounting work needs information about the object and the surface, not just a preferred position. Share the product specifications, dimensions and intended use. Ask the installer to assess the wall or support arrangement and select an appropriate method rather than specifying fixings yourself.

For a television, mirror or shelf, agree the position before drilling and explain any known services or constraints nearby. Do not ask the worker to proceed if the supporting surface or hidden services are uncertain. The company should explain whether further assessment or a different arrangement is needed.

Confirm who supplies the bracket, fixings and accessories. If you provide them, ask the installer to check suitability before booking. Keep the product information and mounting instructions available so the proposed work can be compared with the manufacturer's requirements.

## HDB, condo and access planning

Building rules and access arrangements should be settled before the appointment. Check the relevant [HDB renovation information](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/managing-my-home/renovation-and-maintenance/renovation/important-information-on-renovations) or your condo management's requirements where the task is affected. Ask the company about the work it proposes and the conditions that apply.

Tell the company about parking, lift access, stairs and large packages that need carrying. A quote for assembling furniture in the room may not include moving it up several flights or disposing of the old item. Put those requirements in the initial brief.

For a rented home, obtain the owner's agreement for alterations where needed. If another trade is working nearby, coordinate the sequence. Final [home cleaning](/home-cleaning/) is easier to plan once drilling, assembly and other dusty tasks are complete.

## Check the finished work and keep the details

At handover, inspect the agreed tasks with the worker and ask for the relevant use or care instructions. Confirm that drawers, doors or moving parts operate as intended and that any limitations have been explained. Do not load or use a newly mounted item contrary to the installer's or manufacturer's instructions.

Keep the invoice with the task list, product details and any written workmanship terms. If a part was replaced, record what was supplied. Ask who handles a concern after the visit and whether a return appointment could carry a charge.

Read reviews about similar tasks when comparing companies, and consider the count and source alongside the average rating. HomezyPro provides a starting shortlist; it does not inspect the work or guarantee that a company can complete every task in your home.

## Handyman questions

### Can one company handle my whole list?

Possibly, but ask it to confirm each task and identify any specialist work. A single booking can still involve different qualified people. The quotation should show who handles regulated work, what is included and whether separate visits are needed, rather than treating every household task as interchangeable.

### Should I buy materials before booking?

Confirm the exact items with the company first. Parts that look similar may have different dimensions or compatibility. If you supply a product, share its model and instructions, and agree who handles a missing or unsuitable component. That reduces the chance of an avoidable return visit.

### Is an hourly rate better than a per-job quote?

The better format depends on how clearly the tasks can be defined. An hourly arrangement needs priorities and a limit or approval process; a per-job quote needs a precise scope. Compare the expected total, materials and exclusions rather than assuming either format automatically gives better value.

### Does a handyman quote include disposal?

Only if it is stated. Ask about packaging, old fittings, replaced furniture and any transport needed to remove them. Disposal can be a different task from assembly or repair. Including it in the brief lets companies quote a complete job and helps you prepare the home for the visit.
